医生主导、证据约束的制作流程Clinician-led, evidence-constrained workflow

视觉帮助学习，不作为医学证据；医学主张的来源、适用范围与审批决定均保留在可审计台账中。Visuals support learning but are not evidence; source, scope, and approval decisions for medical claims are retained in an auditable register.

1. Source index

从公开指南入口和本地教学资料索引开始。Start from public guideline entry points and local teaching-source indexes.

2. Claim mapping

把教学陈述拆成 claim、来源和审核状态。Split teaching statements into claims, sources, and review statuses.

3. Visual courseware

用图文解释空间、流程、团队和复评。Use visuals to teach space, flow, teamwork, and reassessment.

4. Clinical review

创伤方向主任医师审批后，按批准范围发布；超出范围的内容必须回到人工审核。After trauma-chief-physician approval, publish only within the approved scope; out-of-scope content returns to human review.

真实病例与隐私Real cases and privacy

真实病例以脱敏文字改编用于教学：泛化年龄与机制，使用相对阶段，省略身份、日历日期、地点、职业、婚育信息和精确资源量。原始 PPT、影像、截图及其元数据未进入公开仓库。课堂问题属于教学设计，不能据此认定原救治存在漏诊或不当。Real cases are adapted into de-identified teaching text: age and mechanism are generalized; stages are relative; identity, calendar dates, location, occupation, reproductive details and exact resource quantities are omitted. Original decks, images, screenshots and metadata are excluded from the public repository. Teaching questions do not establish an actual missed injury or inappropriate care.
