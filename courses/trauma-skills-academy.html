课前测 · 5 题Pretest · five questions

每题 1 分；用于形成性反馈。One point per item; formative feedback.

1. 看到活动性大量外出血，xABCDE 中先突出哪个字母？Which letter highlights active catastrophic external bleeding?
2. 能说话是否等于气道评估结束？Does speaking mean airway assessment is complete?
3. 学员发现新的呼吸异常，下一句报告应包含什么？A learner notices a new breathing abnormality. What should the report contain?
4. 单轮评估未见异常，能否停止复评？Can reassessment stop after one apparently normal assessment?
5. 交接时哪些信息最容易被遗漏？Which information is easily lost at handover?

课后测 · 5 题Posttest · five questions

每题 1 分；用于形成性反馈。One point per item; formative feedback.

1. 敷料迅速浸透，应把哪类线索纳入优先级报告？A dressing rapidly soaks through. Which concern enters the priority report?
2. 说话声音转弱并反应下降，应怎样组织信息？Voice weakens and responsiveness declines. How should findings be organized?
3. 患者表现改变后，哪种报告最便于团队行动？After the patient changes, which report best supports team action?
4. 一次措施后数值改善，接下来如何判断？Values improve after one intervention. What comes next?
5. 检查尚未最终报告，交接应怎样处理？A test has no final report yet. What belongs in handover?
