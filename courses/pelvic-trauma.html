骨盆创伤：隐匿出血风险与团队资源Pelvic Trauma: Occult Bleeding Risk and Team Resources

这不是一张骨盆创伤处置清单，而是一门“先形成问题表征，再逐步获得信息”的临床思维课。学员需要说清已知、可疑、担心、xABCDE 优先级、资源瓶颈和下一次复评。This is not a pelvic-trauma treatment checklist. It is a progressive reasoning lesson: build a problem representation first, then receive new information. Learners state what is known, suspected, feared, prioritized within xABCDE, resource-limited, and due for reassessment.

课程边界Boundary

observe · report · coordinate · reassess

Step 1 · Prehospital notification

只给你院前一句话：第一张团队白板写什么？With one prehospital sentence, what belongs on the first team board?

成年人，高能量道路交通碰撞，撞击模式可能涉及躯干和骨盆。现场信息有限，预计短时间内到院。An adult is involved in a high-energy road collision with possible torso and pelvic loading. Scene information is limited and arrival is expected soon.

参考思路Reasoning reference

较完整的白板会区分机制已知信息、生命威胁与保护状态的未知信息、对隐匿出血和合并伤的担心，以及负责接收交接、记录趋势和更新资源的人。A fuller board separates known mechanism, unknown threat and protection status, concern for hidden bleeding and associated injury, and who receives handover, records trends, and updates resources.

复评问题Reassessment question

到院后哪一条新信息最可能改变当前风险排序？Which new arrival finding could most change the current risk order?

Step 2 · Problem representation

把杂乱信息压缩成一句创伤问题表征Compress fragmented information into one trauma problem representation

参考思路Reasoning reference

问题表征应保留机制、信息不完整、隐匿风险、合并伤和趋势复评，避免把单一影像诊断当成完整的创伤问题。The representation should preserve mechanism, incomplete information, occult risk, associated injury, and trend reassessment rather than reducing trauma to one imaging diagnosis.

复评问题Reassessment question

哪项新发现会让 A、B、C、D 或 E 的排序改变？Which new finding could reorder A, B, C, D, or E?

Step 3 · xABCDE priority

选择需要持续盯住的轴，并说明相互影响Choose the axes requiring sustained attention and explain their interaction

参考思路Reasoning reference

本病例重点是 C/E，但不能把它们与 A/B/D 或系统容量割裂。任何新的呼吸、意识、体征或资源信息都可能改变排序。C/E is the teaching focus, but it cannot be separated from A/B/D or system capacity. New breathing, mental-status, examination, or resource information may change the order.

复评问题Reassessment question

谁负责说出“患者变化”和“系统变化”各是什么？Who states the patient change and the system change?

Step 4 · Team task map

优先级形成后，把下一步写成团队任务After prioritization, convert the next step into team tasks

只写观察、报告、资源协调和复评，不写个人独立操作步骤。Write observation, reporting, resource coordination, and reassessment, not independent procedure steps.

参考思路Reasoning reference

团队任务可包括持续记录趋势、结构化报告不确定信息、同步检查与下游资源状态、明确下一次复评时间点和责任人。具体临床行动由资深团队依据指南和本地流程决定。Team tasks may include trend recording, structured reporting of uncertainty, synchronizing investigation and downstream resource state, and naming the next reassessment point and owner. Qualified teams decide clinical actions under guidelines and local policy.

复评问题Reassessment question

资源没有到位时，哪些未知信息必须继续保留在共享白板？While resources remain pending, which unknowns stay on the shared board?

到院信息逐步释放Progressive Arrival Information

每释放一张卡，学员都要重新说一次优先级和下一次复评。After each card, learners restate priorities and the next reassessment.

1 · 总貌与交接1 · Overall view and handover

患者能够交流，但疼痛明显。院前记录提供了机制信息，骨盆风险、搬动过程和趋势记录仍不完整。The patient can communicate but has marked pain. Prehospital notes provide mechanism, while pelvic risk, movement history, and trend documentation remain incomplete.

2 · 循环趋势2 · Circulation trend

连续观察中，皮肤灌注表现和整体状态出现令人担心的趋势；没有单一数值可以替代团队综合判断。Repeated observation shows a concerning trend in skin perfusion and overall state; no single value replaces team synthesis.

3 · 暴露与骨盆区域3 · Exposure and pelvic region

下躯干疼痛和活动受限更加明确，体表没有足以解释全部循环担心的明显外出血。Lower-trunk pain and movement limitation become clearer, while visible external bleeding does not explain the full circulatory concern.

4 · 合并伤扫描4 · Associated-injury sweep

胸部、腹部、脊柱、四肢和神经系统仍有未完成的信息。骨盆风险不能让团队停止寻找其他出血或损伤来源。Chest, abdomen, spine, limbs, and neurologic information remain incomplete. Pelvic concern must not stop the search for other injury or bleeding sources.

5 · 检查结果的边界5 · Limits of test results

床旁信息没有显示某些腔隙的明确异常，但这不能自动排除腹膜后或骨盆相关出血担心。Bedside information does not show a clear abnormality in some spaces, but this does not automatically exclude retroperitoneal or pelvic bleeding concern.

6 · 资源状态变化6 · Resource-state change

影像、血库、手术/介入和转运资源的可用性不同步。团队需要同时更新患者优先级与系统瓶颈。Imaging, blood bank, surgical/procedural, and transfer availability are not synchronized. The team must update both patient priority and system bottlenecks.

形成团队简报，并做双层复评Build a Team Brief and Perform Dual Reassessment

S · Situation

高能量钝性伤，存在骨盆与隐匿出血风险，当前信息仍不完整。High-energy blunt trauma with pelvic and occult-bleeding concern; information remains incomplete.

B · Background

说明机制、院前趋势、保护/搬动信息、已完成评估和仍未知内容。State mechanism, prehospital trend, protection/movement information, completed assessment, and remaining unknowns.

A · Assessment

区分已知、可疑和担心；说明 C/E 重点及 A/B/D 和合并伤是否改变排序。Separate known, suspected, and feared; state the C/E focus and whether A/B/D or associated injury changes priority.

R · Resources / Reassessment

说清需要同步的资源、当前瓶颈、下一次患者复评和系统复评责任人。State resources to synchronize, current bottlenecks, and owners of the next patient and system reassessment.

参考结构Reference structure

先说问题表征，再说趋势和未知；随后说明 xABCDE 重点、已完成的信息节点、资源瓶颈，以及下一次患者/系统复评由谁完成。Lead with the problem representation, then trend and unknowns. State xABCDE focus, completed information nodes, resource bottlenecks, and ownership of the next patient/system reassessment.

复评问题Reassessment question

简报结束后，接收者能否复述当前最担心的问题和下一次更新点？Can the receiver restate the leading concern and the next update point?

  • 呼吸与灌注趋势Breathing and perfusion trend
  • 新体征与新症状New findings and symptoms
  • 团队行动后的变化Change after team action
系统复评System reassessment
  • 人员、空间和设备Staff, space, and equipment
  • 影像与下游能力Imaging and downstream capacity
  • 血液与手术/介入资源Blood and surgical/procedural resources
