01 · 为什么危险Why it is dangerous

骨盆像一个能装下大量血液的碗The pelvis is a bowl that can hold a lot of blood

高能量骨盆环损伤可在体表看不到血的情况下导致失血性休克。大部分出血来自骨折面和静脉丛，少部分来自动脉——所以“先关上碗”很重要。High-energy pelvic ring injuries can cause shock with no visible blood. Most bleeding comes from fracture surfaces and venous plexus, a minority from arteries — so close the bowl first.

出血来源Bleeding source

约 85% 来自骨、软组织或大静脉；动脉出血约占少数但常更凶险。About 85% from bone, soft tissue or large veins; arterial bleeding is a minority but often brisker.

合并伤Associated injuries

不稳定骨盆骨折患者中多达 90% 伴其他严重损伤：胸、腹、颅脑、长骨。Up to 90% of unstable pelvic fractures have other significant injuries.

死亡率Mortality

10–15% 到急诊时已休克，其中约三分之一死亡；近期报道死亡率约 32%。10–15% arrive in shock; about one third of them die (recent reports ~32%).

WSES：约 85% 的骨盆出血源自骨、软组织或主要静脉结构；死亡主要由不可控出血和生理衰竭造成。WSES: about 85% of pelvic bleeding originates from bone, soft tissue or major veins; deaths come mainly from uncontrolled bleeding and physiological exhaustion.

Coccolini F, et al. WSES classification and guidelines. World J Emerg Surg 2017;12:5

02 · 识别Recognise

认出“不稳定”：用数字而不是感觉Define “unstable” with numbers, not feelings

满足下列任一项，就按血流动力学不稳定处理，启动骨盆带、输血与止血资源。Any one of these means haemodynamically unstable: binder, blood and haemorrhage-control resources now.

查体Exam

看会阴/阴囊血肿、下肢短缩外旋、尿道口出血；骨盆挤压分离试验最多一次，怀疑时不做。Look for perineal/scrotal haematoma, shortened externally rotated leg, blood at meatus; spring the pelvis once at most, not at all if suspected.

影像Imaging

抢救室骨盆正位片 + eFAST；稳定者做多期增强 CT 看造影剂外溢。AP pelvis + eFAST in the bay; multiphase contrast CT if stable to look for blush.

别被骗Don’t be fooled

FAST 阴性不能排除腹膜后/骨盆出血。A negative FAST does not exclude retroperitoneal or pelvic bleeding.

中国专家共识（2015）：怀疑骨盆骨折且血流动力学不稳定者不应过多搬动，禁忌行骨盆挤压-分离试验，尽早使用骨盆带。Chinese consensus (2015): in unstable patients with suspected pelvic fracture, minimise movement, avoid compression-distraction testing, apply a binder early.

03 · 骨盆带Binder

骨盆带：以股骨大转子为中心，不是髂嵴Pelvic binder: centre on the greater trochanters, not the iliac crests

骨盆带通过减少骨盆容积、稳定骨折端来减少出血。最常见的错误是绑得太高（在腰部/髂嵴），几乎没有作用。A binder reduces pelvic volume and stabilises fragments. The commonest error is placing it too high (waist/iliac crests), where it barely works.

位置Position

触摸双侧大转子，带子中线对准大转子；双膝内旋并拢、用绷带固定膝踝。Palpate both trochanters and centre the binder there; internally rotate and tie knees and ankles.

临时替代Improvised

没有专用骨盆带时，可用床单在同一水平环绕收紧并钳夹固定。Without a device, wrap a sheet at the same level and clamp it.

时间与皮肤Time & skin

尽早过渡到确定性固定；骨盆带不宜超过 24–48 小时，定时检查皮肤压伤。Move to definitive fixation early; keep binders ≤24–48 h and check skin for pressure injury.

欧洲指南 R20：怀疑骨盆骨折时院前即辅助使用骨盆带（1C）；骨盆环破裂合并失血性休克者立即闭合并稳定骨盆环（1B）。European guideline R20: adjunct pre-hospital binder for suspected pelvic fracture (1C); immediate ring closure and stabilisation in haemorrhagic shock (1B).

European guideline 6th ed. (2023) R20 · WSES 2017 · NICE NG37 (2016)

WSES 提醒：骨盆带过紧或长时间持续压力（>9.3 kPa 超过 2–3 小时）会增加皮肤坏死和压疮风险。WSES warns that over-tightening or continuous pressure (>9.3 kPa for >2–3 h) raises the risk of skin necrosis and pressure ulcers.

04 · 分型Classification

分型：Young-Burgess 看机制，WSES 看生理Classify: Young-Burgess for mechanism, WSES for physiology

Young-Burgess 告诉你力从哪里来、哪里会破；WSES 分级把骨折形态和血流动力学结合起来，直接对应处理路径。Young-Burgess tells you where the force came from and what breaks; WSES combines anatomy with haemodynamics and maps to a pathway.

APCAPC

前后挤压，“开书样”，骨盆容积增大，出血风险高（APC-II/III）。Anteroposterior compression, “open book”: volume increases, high bleeding risk (APC-II/III).

LCLC

侧方挤压，最常见；老年人低能量 LC 也可能大出血（尤其服抗凝药）。Lateral compression, commonest; even low-energy LC can bleed in older adults, especially on anticoagulants.

VS / CMVS / CM

垂直剪切多见于高处坠落，半骨盆上移；混合机制并存。Vertical shear from falls with hemipelvis displacement; combined mechanisms coexist.

WSES 分级中，只要血流动力学不稳定，无论骨折类型均为 IV 级（重度）。In WSES grading, any haemodynamically unstable patient is grade IV (severe), whatever the fracture pattern.

05 · 止血路径Haemorrhage control

按路径止血：填塞、栓塞、REBOA 各有位置Follow the pathway: packing, embolisation and REBOA each have a place

不稳定患者不去 CT，而是去能止血的地方。没有 24 小时介入能力的医院，腹膜外骨盆填塞联合外固定是重要选择。Unstable patients go to where bleeding stops, not to CT. Where 24-hour IR is unavailable, preperitoneal packing with external fixation is key.

腹膜外填塞Preperitoneal packing

骨盆出血致不稳定时均应考虑，尤其无介入条件的医院（WSES 1C）；须与骨盆稳定联合。Consider in all pelvic-related instability, especially without IR (WSES 1C); combine with stabilisation.

血管栓塞Angioembolisation

CT 见动脉造影剂外溢，或填塞/固定后仍不稳定时考虑。For arterial blush on CT, or persistent instability after packing/fixation.

REBOAREBOA

只是“通往手术或栓塞的桥”，不是确定性止血；Zone III 用于骨盆出血。Only a bridge to surgery or embolisation, not definitive control; Zone III for pelvic bleeding.

WSES：REBOA 被定位为“通往手术的桥梁”；中国共识（2015）也建议极不稳定者在急诊室稳定骨盆、腹膜外填塞和/或主动脉球囊阻断后立即送导管室。WSES positions REBOA as a bridge to surgery; the 2015 Chinese consensus advises stabilisation, packing and/or balloon occlusion in the ED, then straight to angiography for extreme instability.

06 · 合并伤Associated injuries

别只看骨盆：尿道、直肠、阴道和开放伤口Look beyond the bone: urethra, rectum, vagina and open wounds

骨盆骨折常合并泌尿生殖和直肠损伤。开放性骨盆骨折死亡率更高，需要尽早识别和多学科处理。Pelvic fractures often involve genitourinary and rectal injury. Open pelvic fractures carry higher mortality and need early MDT care.

尿道Urethra

尿道口出血、会阴血肿、男性前列腺上移 → 先逆行尿道造影，不盲插导尿管；必要时耻骨上造瘘。Blood at meatus, perineal haematoma, high-riding prostate → retrograde urethrogram before catheter; suprapubic if needed.

直肠/阴道Rectum/vagina

检查有无出血或骨折刺破；开放伤需早期抗生素、清创，必要时粪便转流。Check for blood or penetrating fragments; open injuries need early antibiotics, debridement, possibly diversion.

老年人Older adults

低能量跌倒也可大出血；询问抗凝/抗血小板药并尽早逆转。Low-energy falls can bleed; ask about anticoagulants/antiplatelets and reverse early.

WSES：高等级损伤中胸腹合并伤可达 80%；膀胱、尿道（1.6–25%）、阴道、直肠等损伤在会阴血肿或广泛软组织破坏时应高度怀疑。WSES: thoraco-abdominal injuries in up to 80% of high-grade cases; suspect bladder, urethra (1.6–25%), vagina and rectum with perineal haematoma or major soft-tissue disruption.
