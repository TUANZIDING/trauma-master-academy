Step 1 · Arrival representation

到院初始信息应怎样浓缩？How should the arrival information be compressed?

一名高龄女性在多发伤后由院前转入抢救室。到院即见低灌注、低氧和呼吸增快，右上肢已固定，出血来源和合并伤尚未完整。An older woman arrives after polytrauma with poor perfusion, low oxygenation, and increased respiratory effort. The right upper limb is already stabilized, while bleeding source and associated injuries remain incomplete.

参考表征：高龄多发伤患者，到院即有显著 C/B 轴担心，需同时寻找胸、腹、骨盆及其他隐匿出血来源，并将患者趋势与资源到位情况放在同一条时间轴上。Reference representation: an older polytrauma patient with major C- and B-axis concern on arrival, requiring simultaneous consideration of chest, abdominal, pelvic, and other occult bleeding sources while patient trend and resource readiness are tracked on one timeline.
