Round 1 · Initial notification

院前只有不完整信息Incomplete prehospital information

一名成年骑行者与机动车发生高能量碰撞，疑似被抛出。院前意识模糊；已完成四肢支具固定并建立一条外周静脉通路。院前生命体征趋势、完整 xABCDE 评估及颈椎/骨盆保护状态未能在交接中补齐。An adult rider sustains a high-energy collision with a motor vehicle and may have been ejected. Mental status is altered. Prehospital care includes support of all four limbs and one peripheral IV. Vital-sign trends, a complete xABCDE assessment, and the status of cervical-spine and pelvic protection are not closed in handover.

先肯定已完成的肢体支持和静脉通路，再指出不完整之处：连续生命体征、气道与呼吸、灾难性出血、神经状态、全身暴露、颈椎/骨盆保护和预警内容。目标不是追责，而是把未知变成到院后的并行任务。Acknowledge the limb support and IV access, then identify the missing serial vital signs, airway and breathing status, catastrophic bleeding assessment, neurology, exposure, cervical-spine and pelvic protection, and pre-alert content. The aim is not blame; it is to convert unknowns into parallel arrival tasks.

下一次复评：Next reassessment: 到院交接能否补齐趋势、已做处置和保护状态？Can arrival handover close the trends, treatment-so-far, and protection gaps?

Round 2 · Arrival appearance

初始表象可能令人误判The first snapshot may mislead

入室时意识模糊、躁动并发出呻吟；血压 223/110 mmHg，心率约 60 余次/分。双侧瞳孔约 2.5 mm，等大，对光反射存在。头部及双膝可见裂伤，双腕和双下肢已有支具，多处肢体损伤明显。这仍只是单个时点。On arrival the patient is confused, agitated, and groaning. Blood pressure is 223/110 mmHg and heart rate is in the low 60s/min. Pupils are approximately 2.5 mm bilaterally, equal and reactive. Scalp and bilateral knee wounds are visible; both wrists and lower limbs are supported, with obvious injuries at multiple limb sites. This remains one time point.

单次偏高读数不能等同于循环稳定。问题表征应强调高能量机制、意识异常、多部位开放伤和缺少趋势，等待重复测量与全身评估。One elevated reading does not equal circulatory stability. The representation should emphasize high-energy mechanism, altered consciousness, multiple open wounds, and the absence of a trend pending repeat measurement and whole-patient assessment.

下一次复评：Next reassessment: 下一次循环测量与意识表现是否发生方向性变化？Do the next circulatory measurement and mental status change direction?

Round 3 · Abrupt change

数分钟后出现明显低灌注表现Marked hypoperfusion appears minutes later

约 5 分钟后重复测量：血压 53/35 mmHg，心率 89 次/分，呼吸 26 次/分，SpO₂ 99%，体温 36.5℃；意识障碍随后进行性加深。左大腿远端开放伤有持续暗红色渗出，经加压后可控；没有敷料快速浸透或无法控制的大量外出血。About five minutes later, repeat observations are BP 53/35 mmHg, HR 89/min, RR 26/min, SpO₂ 99%, and temperature 36.5°C; mental status subsequently becomes progressively reduced. A distal thigh wound has persistent dark-red oozing that responds to pressure, without rapidly soaked dressings or uncontrolled major external hemorrhage.

可控制的渗血仍是事实，但不足以独立解释全部循环变化。多发长骨和软组织损伤可进入隐性失血讨论；胸、腹、骨盆、血管及其他外部来源仍需保持开放。Controllable oozing remains a fact but does not independently explain the entire circulatory change. Multiple long-bone and soft-tissue injuries belong in the hidden-loss discussion, while chest, abdomen, pelvis, vascular injury, and other external sources remain open.

下一次复评：Next reassessment: 床旁检查回答了什么，又没有回答什么？What does bedside testing answer, and what does it leave unanswered?

Round 4 · Bedside information

阴性结果不能关闭病例Negative findings do not close the case

床旁超声未见明确大量胸腹腔游离液体或明显气胸征象；尿液未见肉眼血尿，直肠检查未见血染。患者意识、循环和瞳孔仍在动态变化。Bedside ultrasound shows no clear large-volume thoracoabdominal free fluid or obvious pneumothorax sign; there is no gross hematuria or blood staining on rectal examination. Mental status, circulation, and pupils continue to change.

这些结果降低了部分大容量胸腹腔积液的床旁线索，但床旁超声敏感性有限，也不回答长骨、软组织或主动脉损伤。此时需要把生理趋势与后续影像、检验和重复查体整合。These findings reduce some bedside evidence for large thoracoabdominal collections, but ultrasound sensitivity is limited and it does not answer long-bone, soft-tissue, or aortic injury. Physiology now needs integration with imaging, laboratory trends, and repeat examination.

下一次复评：Next reassessment: 患者是否具备离开抢救室检查时的团队监护与应急准备？Does the team have monitoring and contingency readiness for transport out of the trauma bay?

Round 5 · Interrupted imaging

CT途中出现新的A/D风险A new A/D risk interrupts CT

初步支持后收缩压回升至约 110 mmHg，团队在监护下开始 CT。检查进行中患者呕吐，检查被中断；团队返回抢救室完成气道管理后，再评估并继续完成 CTA。After initial support, systolic pressure improves to approximately 110 mmHg and CT begins with monitoring. The patient vomits during the study, which is interrupted. The team returns to the trauma bay for airway management, then reassesses and later completes CTA.

不能声称呕吐一定可防，但可以在离开抢救室前显式评估意识与气道保护能力，给转运设置“暂停条件”，明确随行责任人，携带与风险匹配的监护和应急能力，并向 CT 团队交接当前 A/C/D 风险。任何变化都应中断原计划并重新评估。Vomiting cannot be claimed to have been fully preventable. Risk can still be reduced by explicitly assessing consciousness and airway protection before departure, defining pause conditions, naming an escort with ownership, matching monitoring and rescue capability to the risk, and handing current A/C/D concerns to the CT team. Any change should interrupt the plan and trigger reassessment.

下一次复评：Next reassessment: 气道、氧合、意识、循环和转运应急能力是否已重新闭环？Have airway, oxygenation, mental status, circulation, and transport contingency capability been closed again?

Round 6 · CTA result

确认主动脉损伤，但没有破裂证据Aortic injury is confirmed without evidence of rupture

CTA 正式描述：主动脉弓、降主动脉胸段壁内血肿形成，伴局限性夹层。报告未描述破裂。同期头颅影像未显示能够单独解释全部神经变化的大量颅内出血；检查后双侧瞳孔一度约 5 mm，对光反射存在但较迟钝。Formal CTA description: intramural hematoma of the aortic arch and descending thoracic aorta with localized dissection. Rupture is not described. Concurrent head imaging shows no large intracranial hemorrhage that independently explains the entire neurologic change; after imaging, both pupils are transiently approximately 5 mm with present but less brisk light responses.

急诊团队的任务不是单纯“等会诊”。应持续复评 xABCDE 与瞳孔/意识趋势，明确报告说了什么及没说什么，继续评估多源失血，立即组织创伤、血管/胸部、影像、麻醉、重症与手术资源讨论，并为等待期设置责任人和再升级触发点。不将夹层直接写成已破裂或唯一休克来源。The emergency team is not simply waiting for consultation. Continue xABCDE and serial pupil/mental-status reassessment; state what the report does and does not show; keep a multisource hemorrhage assessment open; convene trauma, vascular/thoracic, imaging, anesthesia, critical-care, and operative resources; and assign ownership and re-escalation triggers during the waiting period. Do not convert dissection into documented rupture or the sole source of shock.

下一次复评：Next reassessment: 新诊断如何同时改变患者风险与系统资源优先级？How does the new diagnosis change both patient risk and system-resource priority?

Round 7 · Relative stability, active system

患者趋稳，系统不能停止The patient trends toward stability; the system must keep moving

CTA 完成后到进入手术室前，循环和呼吸总体趋稳，瞳孔逐渐恢复；患者仍处于插管状态，神经系统评估需考虑当时气道/镇静状态的影响。“趋稳”只表示当前趋势，不代表主动脉、失血或多发骨折问题已结束。From CTA completion to operating-room transfer, circulation and breathing are generally stable and the pupils gradually improve. The patient remains intubated, so neurologic assessment must account for the airway and sedation context. “Trending stable” describes the current trajectory; it does not mean the aortic injury, blood-loss concern, or multiple fractures are resolved.

患者复评包括循环、呼吸、意识、瞳孔、伤口和肢体状态；系统复评包括决定状态、责任人、手术能力、备血、转运与应急方案。瞳孔与循环同步改善只是时间关联，不能写成已经证实的因果。Patient reassessment includes circulation, breathing, mental status, pupils, wounds, and limb status. System reassessment includes decision status, ownership, operative capability, blood-product readiness, transport, and contingency planning. Parallel improvement in pupils and circulation is temporal association, not proven causation.

下一次复评：Next reassessment: 等待期间若任一生理趋势改变，谁负责重新启动团队讨论？If any physiologic trend changes while waiting, who reopens the team discussion?

Round 8 · Definitive-capability handover

转入手术与重症监护交接Transfer to operative and critical-care capability

患者约于凌晨 04:30 进入手术室，05:00–06:45 完成胸主动脉腔内修复（含左锁骨下动脉保留相关处理）。术后仍处于气管插管状态并转入 EICU。本轮只用于交接和资源时间轴教学，不展示支架规格、用药、压力参数或手术操作步骤。The patient enters the operating room at approximately 04:30 and undergoes thoracic endovascular aortic repair from 05:00 to 06:45, including a left-subclavian-preservation component. The patient remains intubated after surgery and transfers to the EICU. This release teaches handover and the resource timeline only; it does not show device specifications, medications, pressure targets, or procedural steps.

延伸学习：术中与术后发生了什么？Extension: What happened intraoperatively and afterward?

术中概览：以腔内方式隔绝胸主动脉损伤，并确认主要分支显影与访问侧肢体灌注。这是结局摘要，不是手术教程。Intraoperative overview: the thoracic aortic injury is excluded endovascularly, with confirmation of major branch visualization and access-limb perfusion. This is an outcome summary, not a procedural tutorial.

术后阶段：患者插管转入 EICU。病情稳定后，当日完成胫骨与股骨阶段性外固定；更后阶段完成双侧股骨及双侧腕部骨折的分期固定。Postoperative phase: the patient transfers intubated to the EICU. After stabilization, staged tibial and femoral external fixation occurs later that day, with later staged fixation of bilateral femoral and wrist fractures.

延伸问题：Extension question: 如何在交接中区分“已完成的最终性能力”和“仍需分期解决的损伤”？How should handover distinguish completed definitive capability from injuries still requiring staged care?

交接应说明机制、早期循环骤变、动态意识和瞳孔、可控制外部渗血、多发长骨损伤、床旁检查边界、CTA确认的主动脉损伤且无破裂证据、已完成的手术、当前气道状态，以及待重症团队继续复评的风险和责任人。Handover should state mechanism, abrupt early circulatory change, dynamic mental status and pupils, controllable external oozing, multiple long-bone injuries, bedside-test limits, CTA-confirmed aortic injury without rupture evidence, completed surgery, current airway status, and the risks and owners requiring continued critical-care reassessment.

复盘问题：Debrief question: 哪一次新信息最明显改变了优先级？哪一次阴性结果最容易造成过早安心？Which new information most changed priority, and which negative finding most invited premature reassurance?
