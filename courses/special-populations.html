01

老年创伤：机制轻，风险未必轻Older adults: low energy does not ensure low risk

  • 补问抗凝/抗血小板用药、基础功能、脆弱性与既往疾病。Clarify anticoagulant/antiplatelet medicines, baseline function, frailty and comorbidity.
  • 低能量跌倒仍可能重伤；体征不典型或心率反应被药物影响时保留担心。Low-energy falls may cause serious injury; retain concern when signs are atypical or medicines alter heart-rate response.
  • 把延迟症状、镇痛与三次评估安排交给明确责任人。Assign ownership for delayed symptoms, analgesia and tertiary review.

02

儿童与妊娠：关注各自的生理Children and pregnancy: physiology changes the assessment

  • 儿童按年龄、体重及儿科规范解读生理，并兼顾保暖、家庭沟通和保护。Interpret pediatric physiology by age/weight and pediatric policy, including warmth, family communication and safeguarding.
  • 孕妇优先母体复苏，及早创伤-产科协作，结合孕周与症状评估母胎风险。Prioritize maternal resuscitation and early trauma-obstetric collaboration, assessing gestation and maternal/fetal concerns.
  • 成人剂量、血压目标和影像路径不能自动移植；按对应来源与本地方案学习。Do not automatically transfer adult doses, BP targets or imaging pathways; use population-specific sources and local policy.

03

烧伤：面积之外，还看什么Burns: what matters beyond visible area

  • 并行查看创伤、吸入损伤担心、深度、关键部位与疼痛。Assess associated trauma, possible inhalation injury, depth, key sites and pain in parallel.
  • 化学/电伤、儿童烧伤及合并伤可需要烧伤专科咨询；避免只凭外观排除风险。Chemical/electrical, pediatric and associated injuries may require burn consultation; appearance alone does not exclude risk.
  • 记录损伤方式、可核查面积/深度、呼吸变化和转运资源，按本地烧伤流程升级。Record mechanism, assessed area/depth, respiratory changes and transfer resources, escalating under local burn policy.

孕妇创伤：母体与产科并行Pregnancy trauma: maternal/obstetric care

母体优先；子宫左推；Rh DMother first; left uterine displacement; Rh D

烧伤：中国新九分法Burns: Chinese rule of nines

成人：9 + 18 + 27 + 46 = 100%Adult: 9 + 18 + 27 + 46 = 100%

头面颈、双上肢、躯干含会阴、双下肢含臀足。Head/face/neck; both arms; trunk including perineum; both legs including buttocks/feet.

12岁以下：头面颈=9+(12−年龄)%；双下肢=46−(12−年龄)%。Under 12: head/neck=9+(12−age)%; both legs=46−(12−age)%.
