01

并行任务，共同目标Parallel tasks, shared goal

  • 控制外出血，寻找隐匿出血；同步协调手术/介入止血资源。Control visible bleeding, assess occult sources and coordinate surgical/interventional resources.
  • 按受伤时间评估 TXA；按本地大出血流程组织血液资源。Assess TXA using injury time and mobilize blood resources under the local hemorrhage protocol.
  • 限制无效大量补液，同时关注体温、离子钙、凝血和灌注反应。Avoid ineffective excessive fluids while tracking temperature, ionized calcium, coagulation and perfusion response.

≤3 h

TXA：从受伤起算TXA: measured from injury

活动或疑似活动性出血时尽早评估使用。NICE：受伤超过 3 小时不常规使用，除非有纤溶亢进证据。Assess early in active or suspected active bleeding. NICE advises against routine use after three hours unless hyperfibrinolysis is evidenced.

80–90 mmHg

允许性低血压：有条件的成人 SBP 目标Permissive hypotension: a conditional adult SBP target

止血前且无脑损伤证据的初期出血情境。颅脑/脊髓损伤不套用；老年、慢性高血压慎重；儿童及孕妇需各自规范。Early bleeding before control without brain-injury evidence. Do not apply to brain/spinal injury; use caution in older adults and chronic hypertension. Children and pregnancy require separate policy.

欧洲指南 2023 · 推荐13European guideline 2023 · R13
SI = HR / SBP

休克指数：看趋势与局限Shock index: trend and limitations

健康成人常见 0.5–0.7；研究中的 0.9–1.0 附近升高提示风险。年龄、药物和病程会影响解释；不能单独决定输血。Often 0.5–0.7 in healthy adults; elevations around 0.9–1.0 indicate risk in studies. Age, medicines and trajectory affect interpretation; SI alone does not determine transfusion.

欧洲指南 2023 · 推荐5European guideline 2023 · R5
ABC ≥2

MTP：评分与临床表现共同判断MTP: combine score and clinical state

ACS TQIP 原文示例：穿透性躯干伤、FAST 阳性、脉搏 >120/min、SBP <90 mmHg，各 1 分。持续不稳定、需手术/介入的活动出血等也需启动评估；不能等累计输血量满足定义。ACS TQIP example: penetrating torso injury, positive FAST, pulse >120/min and SBP <90 mmHg, one point each. Persistent instability and active bleeding requiring intervention also prompt activation assessment; do not wait for a cumulative-volume definition.

ACS TQIP · MTP 启动ACS TQIP · MTP activation

这些是指南条件下的教学锚点；具体启动规则、血液产品、配比和终止标准以本地大出血规范为准。ABC 评分不同文献的边界符号存在版本差异，应核对本地采用版本。These are source-qualified teaching anchors. Local policy defines activation, products, ratios and termination. ABC boundary operators differ across publications; verify the version used locally.

02

输血策略需要随反馈调整Transfusion strategy responds to feedback

  • 大量出血初期按本地固定比例包组织；尽早转向实验室/黏弹性检测指导。Use the local initial fixed-ratio pack, then move to laboratory/viscoelastic-guided therapy as soon as feasible.
  • 区分血浆:红细胞与血浆:血小板:红细胞的写法，核对产品单位。Distinguish plasma:RBC from plasma:platelet:RBC notation and verify product units.
  • 复评活动性出血、灌注、体温和凝血；明确升级与结束 MTP 的责任人。Reassess bleeding, perfusion, temperature and coagulation; assign escalation and MTP termination ownership.
展开教学说明Teaching detail

NICE NG39 对成人活动性出血给出血浆:红细胞 1:1 的起始示例，并要求尽早按凝血结果调整。这不是所有地区、儿童或全血方案的统一配比，不能由本页替代本院血库方案。NICE NG39 gives a starting adult plasma:RBC example of 1:1 with early adjustment to coagulation results. It is not a universal pediatric, whole-blood or regional ratio and does not replace the institutional blood-bank protocol.

可暂停或由教师手动释放信息。运行时每 30 秒进入下一预设阶段；不会评估真实患者，也不计算治疗反应。Pause or let the instructor reveal updates manually. Every 30 seconds advances to a scripted stage; this does not assess real patients or calculate treatment response.
