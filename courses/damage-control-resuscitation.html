01

并行任务，共同目标Parallel tasks, shared goal

  • 控制外出血，寻找隐匿出血；同步协调手术/介入止血资源。Control visible bleeding, assess occult sources and coordinate surgical/interventional resources.
  • 按受伤时间评估 TXA；按本地大出血流程组织血液资源。Assess TXA using injury time and mobilize blood resources under the local hemorrhage protocol.
  • 限制无效大量补液，同时关注体温、离子钙、凝血和灌注反应。Avoid ineffective excessive fluids while tracking temperature, ionized calcium, coagulation and perfusion response.

02

输血策略需要随反馈调整Transfusion strategy responds to feedback

  • 大量出血初期按本地固定比例包组织；尽早转向实验室/黏弹性检测指导。Use the local initial fixed-ratio pack, then move to laboratory/viscoelastic-guided therapy as soon as feasible.
  • 区分血浆:红细胞与血浆:血小板:红细胞的写法，核对产品单位。Distinguish plasma:RBC from plasma:platelet:RBC notation and verify product units.
  • 复评活动性出血、灌注、体温和凝血；明确升级与结束 MTP 的责任人。Reassess bleeding, perfusion, temperature and coagulation; assign escalation and MTP termination ownership.
