高能量交通伤：颈髓风险与左髋关节脱位High-Energy Transport Injury: C-Spine Risk and Left Hip Dislocation

Step 1 · Prehospital story

只给你这些信息：你先怎么想？With only this information, how do you think?

男性青年，骑摩托车与对向车辆发生高能量碰撞，被甩出。院前约半小时送入抢救室。交接中已知建立静脉通路；颈椎保护、髋/骨盆保护和搬动风险状态没有形成清晰闭环。A young adult male was ejected after a high-energy motorcycle collision with an oncoming vehicle. He arrived around half an hour after injury. Handoff notes an intravenous access, but c-spine protection, hip/pelvic protection, and movement-risk status are not clearly closed-looped.

参考思路Reference reasoning

已知：高能量交通伤、被甩出、院前转运、保护状态不完整。可疑：颈椎/颈髓损伤、髋关节脱位或髋臼损伤、多部位软组织/骨折损伤。担心：稳定表象掩盖神经损伤、隐匿出血、搬动导致风险扩大、交接缺口导致复评延迟。Known: high-energy transport injury, ejection, prehospital transfer, incomplete protection status. Suspected: c-spine or cervical cord injury, hip dislocation or acetabular injury, multiple soft-tissue or fracture injuries. Feared: stable appearance hiding neurologic injury, occult bleeding, movement-related risk amplification, and delayed reassessment due to handoff gaps.

复评问题：Reassessment question: 保护状态是否已经被看见、说出、记录并交接？Has protection status been seen, stated, recorded, and handed over?

Step 2 · Problem representation

把病人浓缩成一句创伤问题表征Compress the patient into one trauma problem representation

不要急着命名单一诊断。先把机制、严重程度、保护缺口和可能主导风险说成一句话。Do not rush into a single diagnosis. First state mechanism, severity, protection gap, and possible dominant risks in one sentence.

参考思路Reference reasoning

较好的问题表征会保留“高能量机制 + 多发伤可能 + 保护状态不完整 + 颈髓与髋/骨盆风险 + 需要复评”。它不是正式诊断，也不是治疗路径，而是团队初始共同语言。A stronger representation preserves “high-energy mechanism + possible polytrauma + incomplete protection status + c-spine/cord and hip/pelvic risk + need for reassessment.” It is not a final diagnosis or treatment pathway; it is shared team language.

复评问题：Reassessment question: 这句话是否能帮助团队同时看见神经、骨科和系统交接风险？Does this sentence help the team see neurologic, orthopedic, and system handoff risk together?

Step 3 · xABCDE priority

本病例优先级落在哪些轴？Which axes carry priority in this case?

学员先选择重点轴，再说明理由。这里训练的是关注排序，不是单人处置。Learners choose priority axes and explain why. This trains attention ordering, not independent action.

参考思路Reference reasoning

最应被点亮的是 A/D、E/MSK 和交接复评轴；C 也需要保留为趋势和隐匿风险。B 暂时不是主导矛盾，但不应从共享板上消失。The most visible axes are A/D, E/MSK, and handoff reassessment; C remains as trend and occult-risk language. B is not currently the dominant problem, but it should not disappear from the shared board.

复评问题：Reassessment question: 哪一条新信息会让优先级重新排序？Which new item of information would reorder the priorities?

Step 4 · Team task map

优先级确定后，下一步团队任务是什么？After priority is set, what are the next team tasks?

把任务说成“观察、报告、资源协调、复评”，不要写成独立医嘱。Phrase tasks as observation, reporting, resource coordination, and reassessment, not independent orders.

参考思路Reference reasoning

团队任务可以包括：复核颈椎和髋/骨盆保护状态；记录并重复神经症状趋势；把左髋畸形和搬动风险放入共享板；协调影像、骨科/脊柱相关专科和转运交接讨论；指定下一次复评由谁提出。Team tasks may include: review c-spine and hip/pelvic protection status; record and repeat neurologic symptom trends; place left hip deformity and movement risk on the shared board; coordinate imaging, orthopedic/spine specialty discussion, and transfer handoff; name who prompts the next reassessment.

复评问题：Reassessment question: 团队是否知道“下一次复评看什么、谁来说”？Does the team know “what to reassess next and who will say it”?

入院后体格检查逐步释放Progressive Examination Release

教师可逐张释放信息，让学员不断修正优先级和简报。The instructor can release information card by card so learners revise priorities and briefing.

1 · 总貌、意识与生命体征1 · General appearance, mental status, and vitals

患者清醒，能够对答，疼痛明显；病历记录提示 GCS 约 14，具体评分需复核。脱敏源资料仅保留“生命体征相对平稳”，没有可靠具体数值。The patient is alert and answers questions with prominent pain; charted GCS is around 14 and requires review. The de-identified source retains only that vital signs were relatively stable, without reliable exact values.

“相对平稳”能否让高能量机制和明显疼痛退出风险图？Can relative stability remove high-energy mechanism and prominent pain from the risk map?

2 · 头面部与瞳孔2 · Head, face, and pupils

头面部见血迹和擦挫伤；瞳孔等大等圆、对光反射记录为灵敏。当前瞳孔与对答记录不能取代后续 D 轴和颈椎/颈髓风险复评。Blood staining and abrasions are noted on the head and face; pupils are documented as equal and reactive. Current pupil and response findings do not replace serial D-axis and c-spine or cord reassessment.

哪些神经功能信息还没有检查或没有完整交接？Which neurologic information remains unexamined or incompletely handed over?

3 · 胸腹部与趋势3 · Chest, abdomen, and trend

初始记录未提示胸腹部主导体征，呼吸与循环也未被描述为当时最突出矛盾。这只是一个时点，不能替代重复查体、生命体征趋势与遗漏伤检查。Initial notes do not identify dominant chest or abdominal findings, and breathing and circulation are not described as the most prominent conflicts at that moment. This single time point does not replace repeated examination, vital-sign trend, or missed-injury checks.

4 · 上肢神经查体4 · Upper-limb neurologic examination

双上肢刺痛难忍、触碰敏感且活动受限。这些对称性神经症状比“生命体征暂稳”更能促使团队回到 A/D 轴、颈椎保护与搬动风险。Both upper limbs have severe tingling, touch sensitivity, and limited movement. These symmetric neurologic symptoms should return the team to the A/D axes, c-spine protection, and movement risk despite relatively stable vital signs.

现在你的主导风险和保护状态报告如何改变？How do the dominant risk and protection-status report change now?

5 · 骨盆/髋部与四肢5 · Pelvis, hip, and limbs

左下肢短缩、左髋部突出、压痛且活动受限；右下肢活动相对可。源资料描述肢端血运尚可，但这不取代疼痛、神经功能与搬动前后的重复记录。The left lower limb is shortened with hip prominence, tenderness, and restricted movement; the right lower limb moves relatively better. Distal perfusion is described as preserved, but this does not replace repeated pain, neurologic, and pre/post-movement documentation.

请同时报告髋部畸形、肢端状态和当前搬动风险。Report hip deformity, distal status, and current movement risk together.

6 · 检查后床边复评6 · Post-testing bedside reassessment

检查回来后重新观察、询问和检查患者，是发现双上肢神经症状、左髋畸形和保护交接缺口的关键节点。影像和“已经检查”不能替代床边复评。Re-observing, questioning, and examining the patient after testing reveals bilateral neurologic symptoms, left-hip deformity, and protection-handoff gaps. Imaging and a completed-test label do not replace bedside reassessment.

如果生命体征仍相对平稳，你的风险排序为什么仍然必须改变？Why must risk priority change even if vital signs remain relatively stable?

7 · 趋势复评契约7 · Trend reassessment contract

完整交接不仅要说现在看到什么，还要明确下一次生命体征、意识、呼吸、双上肢神经功能、左下肢与肢端血运由谁、在哪个节点重新记录。A complete handover states not only current findings but also who will re-document vital signs, mental status, breathing, bilateral upper-limb neurologic function, the left lower limb, and distal perfusion at the next checkpoint.

请为这名患者写出一条具体的“下一次复评”交接句。Write one concrete next-reassessment handover sentence.

S · Situation

高能量摩托车交通伤，患者清醒但疼痛明显，存在颈髓风险和左髋/髋臼损伤风险。High-energy motorcycle injury; alert but in prominent pain, with c-spine/cord risk and left hip/acetabular injury risk.

B · Background

院前约半小时入抢救室，交接中保护状态不完整；检查前后需要重新闭环。Arrived around half an hour after injury; protection status was incomplete in handoff and needs closed-loop review before and after testing.

A · Assessment

已知：头面部擦挫伤、双上肢神经症状、左髋畸形和影像异常。可疑/担心：颈髓损伤扩大、髋臼损伤、隐匿损伤和搬动风险。Known: head/facial abrasions, bilateral upper-limb neurologic symptoms, left hip deformity, and abnormal imaging. Suspected or feared: worsening cervical cord injury, acetabular injury, occult injury, and movement risk.

R · Request / Resources

请求团队讨论颈椎保护、神经复评、髋/骨盆保护、影像复核、骨科/脊柱相关专科沟通、转运交接和下一次复评责任人。Ask the team to discuss c-spine protection, neurologic reassessment, hip/pelvic protection, imaging review, orthopedic/spine specialty communication, transfer handoff, and who owns the next reassessment.

最后练习：请你写一句交接简报Final practice: write one handoff brief

参考简报：这是一名高能量摩托车伤患者，入院时保护状态交接不完整。当前患者清醒但双上肢刺痛和活动受限明显，左髋畸形和影像异常提示左髋/髋臼损伤风险。请团队复核颈椎与髋/骨盆保护状态，记录神经趋势，协调影像和相关专科沟通，并明确下一次复评由谁提出。Reference brief: This is a high-energy motorcycle injury with incomplete protection-status handoff. The patient is alert but has prominent bilateral upper-limb tingling and movement limitation, plus left hip deformity and abnormal imaging suggesting left hip or acetabular injury risk. Please review c-spine and hip/pelvic protection status, record neurologic trends, coordinate imaging and relevant specialty communication, and name who prompts the next reassessment.
