总论：为什么先学 xABCDEOverview: Why xABCDE Comes First

住院医师和实习医生常有疾病理论，却缺少进入抢救室后组织信息的方法。xABCDE 在本课件中不是处置算法，而是观察顺序、报告语言、团队协作和复评意识。Residents and interns may know disease theory but lack a method for organizing information in the trauma bay. In this courseware, xABCDE is not a treatment algorithm; it is observation order, reporting language, teamwork, and reassessment awareness.

分层实践Tiered practice

实习识别与报告；规培学习决策时机与监督下关键步骤；青年主治组织并行救治、资源与复评。Interns recognize and report; residents learn decision timing and supervised key steps; early attendings coordinate parallel care, resources and reassessment.

反复使用Repeated use

骨盆、颅脑、胸部、腹部和多发伤模块都会回到同一套语言。Pelvis, TBI, chest, abdomen, and polytrauma modules all return to the same language.

团队而非个人Team, not solo

初学者核心任务是识别风险、准确报告、协助准备、知道何时请上级。The learner's core work is recognizing risk, reporting accurately, assisting preparation, and knowing when to escalate.

审核状态Review state

ATLS/ETM 和本地流程相关表述需合法来源核验和医生终审。ATLS/ETM and local workflow statements require legal source verification and clinician signoff.

01

按威胁重新排序Reprioritise by threat

xABCDE 是共同语言；危及生命的出血与其他立即威胁由团队并行组织，不是做完一项才允许开始下一项。xABCDE provides shared language. Teams coordinate life-threatening bleeding and other immediate threats in parallel, rather than requiring one task to finish before another starts.

02

不给图像过多权力Do not overinterpret an image

疑似张力生理结合临床严重度；FAST 只提供有限的液体信息，不能单独关闭出血假设。Assess suspected tension physiology using clinical severity; FAST offers limited fluid information and cannot independently close the bleeding hypothesis.

03

每次更新有责任人Assign an owner to each update

报告变化、未解问题和下一复评时点；检查后、干预后和转运后重新对比。Report changes, unresolved questions and the next reassessment point; compare again after tests, interventions and transport.

暂停判断：阴性 FAST 能否把 C 判定为完成？Pause and decide: Does negative FAST complete C?

不能。继续寻找其他来源，结合灌注变化和下一步诊断或控制出血路径。No. Continue assessing other sources and perfusion changes while planning appropriate diagnostic or haemorrhage-control steps.

B：重伤氧疗B: oxygen for major trauma

储氧面罩15 L/min，成人危重创伤目标SpO₂ 94–98%；血气和高碳酸血症风险指导后续滴定。以本院规范为准。Reservoir mask 15 L/min; target SpO2 94–98% in critically ill adults, then titrate to blood gases/hypercapnia risk. Follow institutional policy.

氧疗后仍低氧并单侧胸部异常、循环受损：立即评估张力性气胸等可逆威胁，不能只加流量后等待。Persistent hypoxia with unilateral chest findings and circulatory compromise requires immediate assessment for reversible threats such as tension pneumothorax.

A：气道与SMRA: airway and SMR

GCS≤8需评估确定性气道；把通气、氧合、出血复苏与脊柱活动限制（SMR）并行组织。以本院规范为准。GCS at or below eight prompts definitive-airway assessment; coordinate ventilation, oxygenation, hemorrhage resuscitation and spinal motion restriction. Follow institutional policy.

不是仅给一个总GCS；记录E/V/M、瞳孔、污染和保护反射，并复查插管后的波形EtCO₂。Record E/V/M, pupils, contamination and protective reflexes, and confirm intubation with waveform capnography.

C：启动、止血、再评估C: activate, control bleeding, reassess

伤后≤3 h尽早TXA：1 g静脉输注至少10 min，后1 g维持8 h；无TBI出血SBP目标80–90，重型TBI MAP≥80 mmHg。以本院规范为准。Give early TXA within three hours: 1 g IV over at least ten minutes, then 1 g over eight hours; bleeding without TBI SBP 80–90, severe TBI MAP at least 80 mmHg. Follow institutional policy.

HR126/SBP88使SI≈1.43；HR与SBP两项已使ABC≥2，应启动大量输血评估并同步寻找止血路径，不能等CT报告。HR 126/SBP 88 gives SI about 1.43; two ABC criteria meet at least two points: activate MTP assessment while arranging hemorrhage control.
