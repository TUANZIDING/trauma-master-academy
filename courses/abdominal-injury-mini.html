01

不能依赖阴性 eFAST 排除出血FAST answers a limited question

阴性 FAST 不能结束出血风险评估，也不能排除肠、系膜或腹膜后损伤。症状变化时重新查体和更新信息需求。Negative FAST does not close bleeding assessment or exclude bowel, mesenteric or retroperitoneal injury. Re-examine and update information needs as symptoms change.

阴性 ≠ 排除；eFAST不是增强CT的放行门槛。Negative ≠ excluded; eFAST is not the gatekeeper for contrast CT.

能安全检查且需要CT时，不为重复超声拖延；持续失稳无复苏反应时，最少必要影像用于指导紧急止血。When CT is indicated and safe, repeat ultrasound must not delay it; persistent instability without resuscitation response calls for only essential imaging to guide urgent haemostasis.

02

非手术治疗不是无人看护Nonoperative care requires monitoring

选择非手术路径需综合生理稳定、无其他需手术的损伤、连续监测及及时救援能力；不是凭影像分级单独决定。Nonoperative care requires physiologic stability, no other injury requiring surgery, serial monitoring and timely rescue capacity; imaging grade alone is insufficient.

03

新信息改变路径New information changes the pathway

腹痛范围、腹膜刺激征、灌注与实验室趋势恶化时，重新评估延迟显现的损伤；重复 CT 与介入或手术评估按病情和资源决定。Worsening pain, peritoneal findings, perfusion or laboratory trends prompt review for evolving injury; repeat CT and procedural assessment depend on physiology and resources.

暂停判断：阴性 FAST 与持续休克不一致，下一步关注什么？Pause and decide: Negative FAST conflicts with persistent shock. What next?

保留隐匿出血和其他休克机制，复评全身与重复信息；不能以阴性超声证明“没有出血”，也不能为检查延误危急患者的救治。Keep occult bleeding and other shock mechanisms in the differential, reassess the whole patient and obtain appropriate new information. Negative ultrasound neither proves no bleeding nor justifies delaying critical care.

床旁救治与专科接续Bedside care and specialist continuity

每项措施都要回答：为什么做、由谁做、做后看什么。侵入操作由受训获授权人员执行，学员在监督下训练。For every action: why, who and what to reassess. Trained authorised operators perform invasive care; learners practise under supervision.

01

床旁：寻找并控制出血Bedside: find and control bleeding

快速xABCDE、通路、保暖、采血及血液准备并行；复苏反应与隐匿出血部位共同决定下一路径。Run xABCDE, access, warming, blood sampling and blood readiness in parallel; response and occult sources determine the next route.

02

失稳：手术/介入准备Instability: prepare haemostasis

持续不稳定或腹膜炎等需立即外科评估；只有生理和资源允许才进入CT，不能把“先CT”设为固定门槛。Persistent instability or peritonitis warrants urgent surgical assessment; CT requires appropriate physiology and resources, not a fixed prerequisite.

03

稳定：CT、观察或栓塞Stability: CT, observation or embolisation

肝脾非手术管理取决于稳定性、伴随损伤及救援能力；血管损伤讨论栓塞，肠道风险继续查体与选择性复查。Liver/spleen nonoperative care depends on stability, associated injury and rescue capacity; assess vascular injury for embolisation and bowel risk with serial examination and selected repeat imaging.

WSES 指南结构WSES-guided structure
教材式核心Textbook core钝性减速、方向盘/安全带压迫、挤压和穿透伤造成不同损伤谱。早期腹部可以外观平静；意识障碍、脊髓损伤、镇静或分散性疼痛还会降低查体敏感性。因此，机制、连续腹部查体、灌注指标、血红蛋白/乳酸趋势和影像必须共同解释。Blunt deceleration, steering-wheel or seat-belt compression, crush, and penetrating mechanisms create different injury patterns. The abdomen may look quiet early, and altered consciousness, spinal injury, sedation, or distracting pain can reduce examination sensitivity. Mechanism, serial examination, perfusion, hemoglobin/lactate trends, and imaging must be interpreted together.
动态查体Dynamic examination

比较疼痛部位、压痛范围、肌紧张/反跳、膨隆、腹壁瘀斑和肩部牵涉痛是否出现或扩大；记录“本次较上次”的变化。Compare pain location, tenderness extent, guarding/rebound, distension, abdominal-wall bruising, and referred shoulder pain; document change from the previous exam.

影像讨论Imaging discussion

FAST/EFAST 主要寻找特定腔隙液体，阴性不能排除空腔脏器、胰腺、腹膜后或早期少量出血；稳定患者的增强 CT 用于损伤定位、分级和活动性出血线索。FAST/EFAST chiefly detects fluid in selected spaces; a negative study does not exclude bowel, pancreas, retroperitoneal, or small early bleeding. In stable patients, contrast CT localizes and grades injury and identifies active-bleeding clues.

处置路径Management pathway

先按 xABCDE 支持灌注并寻找出血源；是否进入非手术观察、介入或手术路径取决于血流动力学、腹膜炎体征、CT 特征、合并伤和机构即时资源。非手术管理必须包含连续查体、实验室与升级能力。Support perfusion and locate bleeding within xABCDE. Nonoperative observation, interventional, or operative pathways depend on hemodynamics, peritoneal signs, CT features, associated injuries, and immediate resources.
