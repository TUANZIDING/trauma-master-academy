颅脑损伤：从意识变化开始TBI: begin with changing consciousness

意识在下降，先救哪一条生理线？Consciousness is declining: which physiologic threat comes first?

沿用本课合成病例，非真实病历。逐条释放是学习节奏；临床立即威胁并行处理。选择不会改写患者生理。Retained synthetic teaching case, not a patient record. Release structures learning; immediate threats require parallel care. Choices never rewrite physiology.

主诉Presenting complaint
头部撞击后短暂意识丧失，伴反应与语言改变。Brief loss of consciousness after head impact, with altered response and speech.
现病史History
电动车碰撞后头部着地；伤前神经状态、确切伤后时间、抗凝用药及过敏史待核实。Head impact after an e-bike collision; baseline neurology, exact injury time, anticoagulants and allergies need confirmation.
院前交接Prehospital handoff
颈椎保护、氧疗及用药情况原病例未详记；交接时逐项询问，不能假定已完成。Spinal precautions, oxygen and drugs were not fully documented; verify each during handoff rather than assume completion.

01

先纠正可逆因素Address reversible factors

意识改变时并行核查氧合、灌注、血糖、药物和惊厥因素，并评估颅内恶化。When mentation changes, assess oxygenation, perfusion, glucose, drugs and seizures alongside intracranial deterioration.

02

选择有条件的路径Choose a conditional pathway

避免低氧和低血压；颅内监测、重复影像及手术评估结合伤情、趋势与神经外科判断，不能由单张 CT 自动触发。Avoid hypoxaemia and hypotension. Monitoring, repeat imaging and surgical review depend on injury, trajectory and neurosurgical assessment, not one CT alone.

03

复评要可比较Make reassessment comparable

连续记录 GCS 的 E/V/M、瞳孔与四肢反应，同时记录全身生理。新变化比“已经做过 CT”更能提示重新评估。Record GCS components, pupils and limb responses with systemic physiology. New changes require reassessment even after CT.

暂停判断：GCS 下降，但 CT 刚完成，能等下一班吗？Pause and decide: GCS falls soon after CT. Can review wait for the next shift?

不能以检查已完成结束复评。核对混杂因素，同时升级神经评估并明确重复影像的临床触发点。A completed scan does not end reassessment. Check confounders while escalating neurologic assessment and specifying triggers for repeat imaging.

床旁救治与专科接续Bedside care and specialist continuity

每项措施都要回答：为什么做、由谁做、做后看什么。侵入操作由受训获授权人员执行，学员在监督下训练。For every action: why, who and what to reassess. Trained authorised operators perform invasive care; learners practise under supervision.

01

床旁：保护脑与气道Bedside: protect brain and airway

先纠正低氧和低灌注，维持颈椎保护；有分泌物处理困难、通气或气道保护不足时由高级团队评估气道。Correct oxygenation and perfusion while protecting the cervical spine; advanced teams assess airway protection and ventilation when inadequate.

02

恶化：复查与专科Decline: re-examine and escalate

新意识、瞳孔或运动变化立即复评并讨论再影像；CT阴性不能替代持续观察。New mental, pupil or motor changes require urgent reassessment and consideration of repeat imaging; a negative CT does not replace observation.

03

神经外科/重症：条件性升级Neurosurgery / ICU: conditional escalation

占位病变、颅压监测、分层颅压治疗与手术由专科结合临床和CT决定；不把常规过度通气作为普遍措施。Specialists integrate examination and CT for mass lesions, ICP monitoring, tiered therapy and surgery; routine hyperventilation is not a universal measure.

步骤、适用条件与复评核查Sequence, conditions and reassessment checks
  1. 确认主导威胁与当前生理，口头分派操作者和监护者。Confirm the dominant threat and current physiology; name operator and monitor.
  2. 按院内规程准备器材、通路、血液及专科资源；救命处置不等教学问答。Prepare equipment, access, blood and specialist resources under local protocol; rescue care never waits for teaching prompts.
  3. 由获授权团队实施所选措施；必要时结合技能页的专业步骤，不能用流程卡代替操作培训。Authorised teams deliver the selected intervention; linked skills supply procedure teaching, not certification by a flow card.
  4. 立即比较操作前后氧合、灌注与意识，记录时间、氧疗、药物及失败信号。Immediately compare oxygenation, perfusion and consciousness and record time, oxygen, drugs and failure signals.
  5. 没有改善时重新寻找未解决机制，升级专科或替代路径，明确下一复评责任人。If response is inadequate, reassess mechanisms and escalate specialists or fallback pathways with a named next owner.

颅脑损伤深化层：防止继发性脑损伤TBI Depth Layer: Preventing Secondary Brain Injury

把机制、连续神经查体、全身生理、影像变化和团队升级放进同一时间轴。Place mechanism, serial neurologic examination, systemic physiology, imaging evolution, and escalation on one timeline.

ACS TBI 2024ACS TBI 2024
教材式核心Textbook core原发性损伤发生在受力当时，随后低氧、低灌注、通气异常、颅内病变进展、发热和代谢紊乱可加重继发性损伤。学习重点不是背一次 GCS，而是描述每个分项、瞳孔、局灶体征、语言/气道保护能力及其连续变化。Primary injury occurs at impact; subsequent hypoxemia, hypoperfusion, ventilatory disturbance, expanding intracranial lesions, fever, and metabolic derangement can amplify secondary injury. The goal is not one memorized GCS total, but serial description of components, pupils, focal signs, voice/airway protection, and change.
连续神经查体Serial neurologic examination

分别记录睁眼、语言和运动反应，比较双侧瞳孔大小与反应、肢体差异、抽搐、躁动/嗜睡和气道分泌物处理能力；并行核查镇静、低氧、低灌注和低血糖等混杂因素，同时评估颅内恶化。Record eye, verbal, and motor responses separately; compare pupils, limb asymmetry, seizure, agitation/drowsiness, and secretion handling. First consider confounders such as sedation, hypoxemia, hypoperfusion, and hypoglycemia.

影像与趋势Imaging and trend

头颅 CT 用于识别出血、占位效应、骨折和其他急性改变；一次阴性 CT 不能否定全部 TBI，也不能替代神经状态持续恶化时的重新评估。Head CT identifies hemorrhage, mass effect, fracture, and other acute changes. A negative CT does not exclude all TBI and cannot replace renewed evaluation when neurologic status worsens.

团队处置框架Team management frame

同步维持氧合、通气和灌注，保护颈椎，及早识别可逆全身原因；神经恶化、瞳孔改变或影像高风险信息应推动高级监护、神经外科和重复影像等团队升级。Maintain oxygenation, ventilation, and perfusion while protecting the cervical spine and correcting reversible systemic causes. Neurologic decline, pupil change, or high-risk imaging should prompt team escalation to advanced monitoring, neurosurgical review, and repeat imaging as appropriate.

复评句式Reassessment phrase

“与上次相比，E/V/M、瞳孔、局灶体征、气道保护、氧合和循环分别怎样变化？”“Compared with last time, how have E/V/M, pupils, focal signs, airway protection, oxygenation, and circulation changed?”

常见陷阱Common trap

把躁动只归因于“不配合”，或看到一次正常影像后停止观察；两者都会漏掉动态恶化。Attributing agitation to noncooperation or stopping observation after one reassuring image can both miss evolving deterioration.

交接重点Handover focus

报告基线、最近一次变化、时间点、已排除的混杂因素、当前保护状态以及下一次复评责任人。Report baseline, latest change and time, addressed confounders, current protection status, and ownership of the next reassessment.
