01 · 现场分诊Field triage

把对的病人送到对的医院Get the right patient to the right hospital

重伤员越早到达有确定性止血能力的医院越好。现场分诊决定救治链的起点。The sooner a severely injured patient reaches a hospital that can stop bleeding, the better. Field triage sets the chain’s start.

红色标准（举例）Red criteria (examples)

不能听从指令；呼吸 <10 或 >29 次/分；成人收缩压 <90 mmHg；头颈躯干穿透伤；怀疑骨盆骨折；需止血带或填塞的出血。Cannot follow commands; RR <10 or >29; adult SBP <90 mmHg; penetrating head/neck/torso; suspected pelvic fracture; bleeding needing tourniquet or packing.

去哪里Where

直接送往有能力处理的创伤中心；国内参照各地创伤中心分级与 120 调度。Directly to a capable trauma centre; in China follow regional trauma-centre levels and 120 dispatch.

时间Time

目标是缩短“受伤到止血”的时间，而不只是“到医院”的时间。Minimise injury-to-haemorrhage-control time, not just time to hospital.

欧洲指南推荐：重伤员直接转运至合适的创伤机构（1B），并尽量缩短受伤至出血控制的时间（1B）。European guideline: transport severely injured patients directly to an appropriate trauma facility (1B) and minimise time to bleeding control (1B).

European guideline 6th ed. (2023) R1 · ACS COT Field Triage (2021)

02 · 院前预警Pre-alert

预警电话：一分钟，决定接下来十分钟One minute of pre-alert shapes the next ten

院前用 ATMIST 预警；医院据此启动对应级别的创伤团队、通知血库和手术室，并写在共享白板上。Pre-hospital teams pre-alert with ATMIST; the hospital activates the right team, alerts blood bank and theatre, and writes it on a shared board.

必须有Must include

ETA、机制、生命体征趋势、已做处置（止血带时间、TXA、液体）。ETA, mechanism, vital-sign trends, treatment (tourniquet time, TXA, fluids).

医院动作Hospital actions

启动团队、预热房间、准备血制品和骨盆带、通知影像/手术。Activate team, warm room, ready blood and binder, alert imaging/theatre.

常见缺口Common gaps

受伤时间、途中变化、抗凝药使用——明确写“未知”。Time of injury, en-route change, anticoagulants — write “unknown”.

交接信息按固定结构说完，接收方复述关键数字（ETA、血压、止血带时间）。Deliver a fixed structure; the receiver reads back key numbers (ETA, BP, tourniquet time).

ATMIST · NICE NG39 (2016) · ATLS 11

03 · 到院接收Reception

到院：安静交接，然后多人并行Arrival: quiet handover, then parallel work

交接不是念完就结束：接收方要确认已知、未知和当前担心，然后团队按岗位同时开始 xABCDE。Handover is not just reading aloud: confirm known, unknown and current worries, then start xABCDE in parallel.

已知Known

机制、受伤部位、生命体征及趋势、已做处置。Mechanism, injuries, vitals and trends, treatment.

未知Unknown

明确说“尚不清楚”，不用猜测补空白。Say “not known” rather than guessing.

担心Worried

用观察到的变化表达：“途中收缩压从 110 降到 88”。State observed change: “SBP fell from 110 to 88 en route.”

ATLS 第 11 版：致命外出血先控制（xABCDE），初评中发现威胁立即处理，并反复复评。ATLS 11: control exsanguinating haemorrhage first (xABCDE), treat threats as found, reassess repeatedly.

ATLS 11 (2025) · TeamSTEPPS 3.0

04 · 复苏止血Resuscitation

损伤控制复苏：止血、少晶体、早输血、防凝血病Damage control resuscitation: stop bleeding, limit crystalloid, give blood early

失血性休克的核心是尽快止血。在止血前，用允许性低血压、早期平衡输血、氨甲环酸和保温来争取时间，避免“致死三联征”。The core is stopping the bleeding. Until then, buy time with permissive hypotension, early balanced blood, TXA and warming to avoid the lethal triad.

致死三联征Lethal triad

低体温、酸中毒、凝血病，加上低钙血症常被称为“致死菱形”。Hypothermia, acidosis, coagulopathy — with hypocalcaemia, the “lethal diamond”.

输什么What to give

尽早使用血制品，按大量输血方案平衡输注；限制晶体液。Blood products early via a massive transfusion protocol; restrict crystalloid.

别忘了Don’t forget

加温输液、保暖、监测离子钙与凝血（可用黏弹性检测指导）。Warm fluids, keep warm, monitor ionised calcium and coagulation (viscoelastic tests).

氨甲环酸应在伤后 3 小时内尽早给予，不必等待黏弹性检测结果（欧洲指南 R23，1A）。Give TXA as early as possible within 3 h of injury, without waiting for viscoelastic results (EU R23, 1A).

European guideline 6th ed. (2023) R13, R23, R31 · CRASH-2 (Lancet 2010)

05 · 确定性决策Definitive care

去哪里止血：手术室、介入室、CT 还是转院Where to stop the bleeding: OR, IR, CT or transfer

决策取决于生理状态：血流动力学不稳定的患者不应被送去 CT“先看看”，而应去能止血的地方；本院能力不足时尽早转运。Physiology decides: unstable patients should not go to CT “to have a look” but to where bleeding can be stopped; transfer early if capability is lacking.

不稳定Unstable

床旁 eFAST + 胸/骨盆 X 线 → 手术室/杂交手术室；骨盆出血考虑填塞或栓塞。Bedside eFAST + chest/pelvis X-ray → OR/hybrid OR; packing or embolisation for pelvic bleeding.

稳定/有反应Stable/responding

增强 CT（全身 CT）明确损伤，再决定手术、介入或非手术治疗。Contrast CT (whole-body) to define injuries, then operate, embolise or observe.

需转院Transfer

医生对医生直接沟通；不要为了完善检查而推迟转运。Doctor-to-doctor call; do not delay transfer for tests.

ATLS：一旦判断需要转运到确定性治疗，不要因诊断性检查而延误转运。ATLS: once transfer to definitive care is needed, do not delay it for diagnostic tests.

ATLS 11 · Transfer to definitive care · European guideline (2023)
展开：指南原文要点与教学提示Expand: guideline points and teaching notes

转运前核对：气道是否安全、通路是否足够、血制品与药物、氧气与电量、陪同人员资质、接收医院和联系人。ATLS 推荐使用 ABC-SBAR 结构完成转运交接。Before transfer check airway, access, blood and drugs, oxygen and batteries, escort skills, receiving hospital and contact. ATLS recommends ABC-SBAR for transfer handover.

06 · 归处交接Disposition

交给下一个团队：结构化交接 + 24 小时内三次评估Hand over well, then do a tertiary survey within 24 hours

救治链最后一环最容易丢信息：未回报的检查、尚未处理的骨折、止血带时间。交接要点名责任人，并安排三次评估查漏。The last link loses information: pending results, untreated fractures, tourniquet time. Name owners and schedule a tertiary survey.

SBARSBAR

现状、背景、评估、建议；未解决问题单独列出。Situation, background, assessment, recommendation; list open issues.

待办Pending

未回报检查写明“待回报”并指定跟踪人和时间，不写成“未见异常”。Write pending results as pending, with an owner and time — never “normal”.

三次评估Tertiary survey

入院 24 小时内从头到脚再查一遍，发现漏诊损伤。Head-to-toe re-examination within 24 h to catch missed injuries.

ATLS 强调在初评与二次评估之后进行三次评估，以减少漏诊；交接时未解决问题必须有明确责任人。ATLS emphasises a tertiary survey after primary and secondary surveys to reduce missed injuries; open issues need named owners.

ATLS 11 · Secondary survey · I-PASS/SBAR handover

救治链上最常见的 4 个断点Four common breaks in the chain

✕不稳定的患者先送 CT “看清楚”。Sending an unstable patient to CT “to see clearly”.

✓先在床旁用 eFAST 和平片找出血来源，去手术室或介入止血。Use bedside eFAST and X-rays, then go to OR or IR to stop bleeding.

ATLS 11 · WSES

✕等完善所有检查后再联系上级医院。Waiting for all tests before calling the receiving hospital.

✓一旦判断本院能力不足，立即医生对医生联系，边稳定边准备转运。Once local capability is insufficient, call doctor-to-doctor and prepare transfer while stabilising.

ATLS 11

✕大量输注晶体液把血压“补正常”。Pouring crystalloid to “normalise” blood pressure.

✓出血控制前目标收缩压 80–90 mmHg（无颅脑损伤），尽早输血。Target SBP 80–90 mmHg before bleeding control (no TBI) and give blood early.

EU 2023 R13

✕交接写“CT 结果待查”，没有人跟进。“CT pending” with no one following up.

✓写明谁、何时追踪结果，以及结果异常时找谁。State who follows it up, when, and whom to call if abnormal.
