01

抢救记录：时间与变化可追溯Resuscitation records: traceable time and change

  • 记录带时间的机制、初评、连续体征、决策依据、措施和反应。Record timed mechanism, assessment, serial findings, rationale, actions and response.
  • 阳性、阴性、未检查和不可靠结果分开写；注明信息来源。Distinguish positive, negative, unexamined and unreliable findings; identify the source.
  • 病危/病重通知、手术/输血等同意与正式病历使用本院认可文书。Use institutional records for critical-condition notification and surgical/transfusion consent.

02

告知：让患者和家属理解下一步Communication: make the next step understandable

  • 说明当前危险、计划和不确定性，核对理解，约定下次更新。Explain risks, plans and uncertainty, check understanding and agree the next update.
  • 侵入操作沟通涵盖目的、获益风险、替代方案和患者选择。Discuss purpose, benefits/risks, alternatives and patient choice for invasive procedures.
  • 患者决策能力、代理与紧急授权按当地法规和本院制度处理。Capacity, representation and emergency authorization follow local law and institutional policy.

03

交接：把未完成的任务交到人Handover: assign unfinished tasks to people

  • 按情况/背景/评估/建议组织，突出趋势和措施后反应。Organize situation, background, assessment and recommendation, highlighting trends and response.
  • 待回报结果、未检查区域、资源限制、复评时点和接班人逐项核对。Reconcile pending results, unexamined regions, resource limits, review times and the receiving clinician.
  • 用复述确认重点；记录交接时间、双方角色和未解问题。Confirm priorities by read-back; record timing, roles and unresolved concerns.

抢救、告知与交接文书Resuscitation, consent and handover records

抢救结束后 ≤6 h 据实补记并注明以本院规范为准。Truthful, marked supplementation ≤6 h after rescue ends Follow institutional policy.

仅限因抢救急危患者未能及时书写；不是所有病历可延迟。Only when urgent rescue prevented timely writing; not permission to delay all records.

时间到分钟；措施、反应、人员、告知与交接责任。Minute-level time, actions, responses, staff, communication and handover ownership.

签署困难记录原因并走合法授权/急救审批流程，不造签名或回填时间。Record signing difficulties and use lawful emergency authorization; never fabricate signatures/times.

两种补记时限，不能混用Two supplementation deadlines that differ

抢救记录：6 hRescue record: six hours

因抢救未能及时书写，结束后6 h内据实补记并注明；记录时间到分钟。以本院规范为准。If rescue prevents timely writing, supplement truthfully and mark within six hours after rescue; timestamp to minutes. Follow institutional policy.

参加人员姓名及专业技术职称；主持抢救者审核签字。Record participant names and professional technical titles; the rescue leader reviews and signs.

口头医嘱：即刻Verbal order: immediately

仅限急危抢救必要情形；护士复诵，抢救结束后医师即刻据实补记（第28条）。For necessary emergency-rescue orders, the nurse repeats the order; the physician documents it immediately after rescue (Article 28).

不能将医嘱补记推迟到6 h期限；药名、剂量、途径及时间逐项核对。The six-hour record deadline does not extend the order deadline; verify drug, dose, route and time.

交接与急会诊Handover and urgent consultation

交班记录交班前完成；接班记录接班后24 h内完成；急会诊10 min内到场、结束后即刻完成记录。以本院规范为准。Outgoing record before handover; incoming record within 24 hours; urgent consultation arrival within ten minutes and record immediately after consultation. Follow institutional policy.

危重患者床旁交班、专册记录、双方签字；危急值记录、复读确认、立即通知医师并跟踪处理。Hand over critical patients at the bedside, with dedicated records and both signatures; record/read back/confirm critical values, notify the physician promptly and follow through.

紧急签署与输血：按国内流程闭环Emergency consent and transfusion: close the loop

紧急授权和拒绝意见Emergency authorization and refusal

第10条：抢救时法定签署人无法及时签字，按医疗机构负责人或授权负责人签署流程办理，记录原因。Article 10: when timely signing by the lawful decision maker is impossible during rescue, use the institution-head/authorized-head pathway and record why.

输血告知包括目的、成分、替代方法、方式、风险及签署时限；不同意输血也应形成签署的书面记录。Discuss purpose, components, alternatives, method, risk and signing time; document refusal in a signed written record.

即刻输血与评价Immediate transfusion and evaluation

血型无法确认且必须即刻输血，可发O型红细胞和/或AB型血浆；发血单注明“紧急-相容性检测未完成”，发血后即刻开始相容性检测。以本院规范为准。If immediate transfusion is required before blood group confirmation, emergency O red cells and/or AB plasma may be issued; label incomplete compatibility testing and commence it immediately after issue. Follow institutional policy.

输血后24 h内评价成分、剂量、反应和效果，记入病历；未达预期时与输血科共同分析。以本院规范为准。Within 24 hours, document components, dose, reactions and effect; investigate inadequate response with transfusion services. Follow institutional policy.
