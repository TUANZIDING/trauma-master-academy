从接到预警到热复盘：抢救室里的 6 个时刻From pre-alert to debrief: six moments in the trauma bay

每个模块都对应一个真实时刻：看什么、站哪里、说什么，并标出所依据的国际指南。Each module is a real moment: what to look at, where to stand, what to say, and the guideline behind it.

  1. −10 min接预警Pre-alert

    记录 ATMIST，判断是否启动创伤团队。Record ATMIST; decide on trauma-team activation.

  2. −5 min团队简报Team brief

    组长自我介绍、分配角色、说出预案与求助路径。Leader introduces, assigns roles, states the plan and escalation.

  3. −2 min防护与设备PPE & kit

    手套、护目镜、防水衣；吸引开机、加温液体、骨盆带、止血带就位。Gloves, eye protection, gown; suction on, warmed fluids, binder and tourniquet ready.

  4. 0 min安静交接Hands-off handover

    除致命出血外先停手，听完院前交接再过床。Unless exsanguinating, pause and hear the handover before transfer.

  5. 0–10 minxABCDE 并行Parallel xABCDE

    各岗位同时工作，组长每完成一轮大声总结。Roles work in parallel; the leader summarises aloud after each round.

  6. 结束后After热复盘Hot debrief

    5 分钟 STOP5：总结、做得好、待改进、行动项。Five-minute STOP5: summary, went well, improve, actions.

01 · 空间Space

看懂抢救室：每块区域都有固定任务Read the room: every zone has a job

进门先找四样东西：床头气道区、两侧操作区、床尾组长位、通道。站位错了，会挡住别人的生命支持。Find four things first: the airway head, the procedure sides, the leader at the foot, and the access lane. Standing in the wrong place blocks life support.

看什么Look for

吸引器、氧源、气道车、除颤仪、加温输液、超声、血液冰箱/送血路线。Suction, oxygen, airway trolley, defibrillator, fluid warmer, ultrasound, blood route.

站哪里Stand where

初学者站记录员旁或床尾一侧，不站床头和两侧操作位。Learners stand by the scribe or at the foot, never at the head or procedure sides.

说什么Say

“我是实习医生 X，可以负责记录/报生命体征。”“I’m intern X; I can scribe or call vital signs.”

ATLS 第 11 版把“复苏团队的功能与沟通”单列为第 2 章，强调到达前准备好环境与团队。ATLS 11 gives team function and communication its own chapter and stresses preparing room and team before arrival.

02 · 预警Pre-alert

接预警：用 ATMIST 把一通电话变成准备清单Turn the pre-alert call into a checklist with ATMIST

院前信息不完整是常态。按 ATMIST 记录，在白板上分成“已知 / 未知 / 担心”，并据此决定启动哪个级别的团队。Incomplete information is normal. Record ATMIST, split the board into known / unknown / worried, and choose the team level.

A · TA · T

年龄、性别；受伤时间与预计到达时间（ETA）。Age, sex; time of injury and ETA.

M · IM · I

机制（坠落高度、车速、穿透伤）；已发现或怀疑的损伤。Mechanism (fall height, speed, penetration); injuries found or suspected.

S · TS · T

生命体征及变化；已做处置（止血带时间、液体、药物）。Signs and trends; treatment given (tourniquet time, fluids, drugs).

交接格式要固定、简短、按顺序说完；接收方复述关键数字确认。Use a fixed, brief, ordered format; the receiver reads back key numbers.

ATMIST (UK NHS/JRCALC handover) · NICE NG39 Major trauma: service delivery (2016)
展开：指南原文要点与教学提示Expand: guideline points and teaching notes

启动标准各院不同。可参考 ACS COT 2021 现场分诊指南的“红色标准”（如成人收缩压 <90 mmHg、呼吸 <10 或 >29 次/分、不能听从指令、近端肢体离断、怀疑骨盆骨折、需止血带或填塞的活动性出血）作为高级别团队启动的讨论起点。Activation criteria vary. ACS COT 2021 field-triage red criteria (e.g. adult SBP <90 mmHg, RR <10 or >29, unable to follow commands, proximal amputation, suspected pelvic fracture, bleeding needing tourniquet or packing) are a reasonable starting point.

03 · 简报Briefing

到达前 5 分钟：团队简报和个人防护Five minutes before arrival: brief and protect

组长站到床尾，自我介绍，逐个点名分配角色，说出“如果……就……”的预案，并确认每个人戴好防护。The leader stands at the foot, introduces themself, assigns each role by name, states “if… then…” plans and checks PPE.

角色Roles

组长、气道、左/右操作、护士（药物/输血）、记录员、影像技师。Leader, airway, left/right procedures, nurses (drugs/blood), scribe, radiographer.

预案Plans

如：到院即低血压 → 启动大量输血方案并联系手术室；具体阈值按本院流程。E.g. hypotensive on arrival → activate massive transfusion and call theatre; thresholds per local protocol.

防护PPE

标准预防：手套、口罩、护目镜/面屏、防水隔离衣。Standard precautions: gloves, mask, eye protection, fluid-resistant gown.

ATLS 要求所有接触患者的人员采取标准预防；简报的价值在于让每个人在患者进门前就知道自己的任务。ATLS requires standard precautions for everyone in contact; briefing means everyone knows their task before the doors open.

ATLS 11 · TeamSTEPPS 3.0 (AHRQ, 2023)

04 · 到达Arrival

0–2 分钟：先听交接，再过床，然后 xABCDE 并行Minutes 0–2: listen, transfer, then parallel xABCDE

患者进门后，除非有致命外出血需要立刻压迫，全队先停手约半分钟听完交接；过床时保持脊柱活动限制（SMR），然后各岗位同时开始。On arrival, unless exsanguinating bleeding needs immediate pressure, the team pauses briefly for handover; transfer with spinal motion restriction, then all roles start together.

xx

致命外出血：直接压迫、止血带、伤口填塞，最先处理。Exsanguinating bleeding first: pressure, tourniquet, packing.

A–E 并行A–E in parallel

气道医生管 A，操作医生管 B/C，护士建通路采血，组长按顺序收集报告。Airway doctor on A, procedure doctors on B/C, nurses on access and bloods; the leader collects reports in order.

复评Reassess

每次干预后、病情变化或转运前，回到 x 重新走一遍。After each intervention, any change, or before transfer, go back to x.

ATLS 第 11 版（2025）：初评由 ABCDE 改为 xABCDE，致命性外出血控制先于气道处理。ATLS 11th ed. (2025): ABCDE became xABCDE; control exsanguinating haemorrhage before airway.

ACS ATLS 11 (2025) · European guideline on bleeding, 6th ed. (2023), R2
展开：指南原文要点与教学提示Expand: guideline points and teaching notes

欧洲大出血与凝血病管理指南（2023）推荐：开放伤口局部压迫控制致命出血（1B），手术前可辅助使用止血带控制肢体致命出血（1B）。The 2023 European bleeding guideline recommends local compression of open wounds (1B) and adjunct tourniquets for life-threatening extremity bleeding before surgery (1B).

05 · 沟通Communication

闭环沟通：指令要点名、复述、报告完成Closed-loop communication: name, read back, report done

抢救室最常见的错误不是不会做，而是没人听见、做了两遍或没人做。每条指令点名一个人，对方复述，执行后报告。The commonest failure is not skill but communication: unheard, duplicated or undone. Name one person, they read back, then report completion.

发出Send

“李护士，给 1 g 氨甲环酸，10 分钟静推完。”“Nurse Li, give 1 g tranexamic acid over 10 minutes.”

复述Read back

“氨甲环酸 1 g，10 分钟，明白。”“Tranexamic acid 1 g over 10 minutes, understood.”

关闭Close

“氨甲环酸 1 g 已开始，09:12。”记录员写入时间。“TXA 1 g started at 09:12.” The scribe logs the time.

任何人发现风险都可以叫停：用 CUS——“我担心（Concerned）……我不安（Uncomfortable）……这是安全问题（Safety）”。Anyone can stop the line with CUS: “I’m Concerned… Uncomfortable… this is a Safety issue.”

TeamSTEPPS 3.0 (AHRQ, 2023) · ATLS 11 Ch. 2

组长在每轮 xABCDE 后用 10 秒做“小结”：目前发现、当前担心、下一步、谁负责——让所有人重新对齐。After each xABCDE round the leader takes ten seconds to summarise: findings, concerns, next steps, owners.

06 · 复盘Debrief

结束后 5 分钟：用 STOP5 做热复盘Five minutes after: a STOP5 hot debrief

病人离开抢救室后，团队用 5 分钟复盘，把经验留下来，而不是只留下疲惫。When the patient leaves, take five minutes so the team keeps the lessons, not just the fatigue.

S · TS · T

Summarise 总结病例；Things that went well 做得好的。Summarise the case; Things that went well.

O · PO · P

Opportunities to improve 可改进点；Points to action 具体行动与负责人。Opportunities to improve; Points to action with owners.

55

控制在 5 分钟，对事不对人。Keep it to five minutes; discuss systems, not blame.

STOP5 是急诊科热复盘工具：总结、做得好、待改进、行动项，约 5 分钟完成。STOP5 is an ED hot-debrief tool: summary, positives, improvements, actions, in about five minutes.

初学者最常见的 4 个错误Four common beginner mistakes

✕三个人同时围在床头处理气道，没人建通路。Three people crowd the airway; nobody gets access.

✓组长按岗位分工：一人管气道，其他人回到 B/C 和记录位。The leader assigns roles: one on airway, others back to B/C and scribing.

ATLS 11 Ch. 2

✕患者进门就开始剪衣服、抽血，交接没人听完。Cutting clothes and drawing blood while no one hears the handover.

✓除致命出血外，先安静听完 ATMIST，再过床开始操作。Unless exsanguinating, hear ATMIST first, then transfer and start.

ATMIST · NICE NG39

✕口头医嘱只回答“好的”。Answering a verbal order with just “OK”.

✓复述药名、剂量、途径，执行后报告时间。Read back drug, dose and route; report the time when given.

TeamSTEPPS 3.0

✕反复做骨盆挤压试验“确认”骨折。Repeated pelvic springing to “confirm” a fracture.

✓怀疑骨盆骨折时不做挤压分离试验，直接上骨盆带并拍片/超声。If pelvic fracture is suspected, skip springing; apply a binder and image.

01

准备不是设备清单Preparation is more than equipment

到院前确认空间可达、监护可用、团队角色与求助路径；具体编制按本地流程。Before arrival confirm access, monitoring readiness, team roles and escalation paths; staffing follows local workflows.

02

交接分清事实与担心Separate facts from concerns

报告机制、已知生理变化、已做事项和未知信息，不把猜测写成观察事实。Report mechanism, known physiologic changes, completed actions and unknowns; do not turn assumptions into observations.

03

核查不延误急救Checks should not delay urgent care

核查帮助团队发现遗漏；立即威胁仍按优先级并行处理，交接后有人持续复评。Checks help identify omissions. Immediate threats remain prioritised and coordinated, with an assigned reassessment owner after handoff.
