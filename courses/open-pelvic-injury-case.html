    本轮问题Discussion prompt

  3. 3/4 · MDT：止血与脏器处理并行MDT: hemorrhage control and organ management

This describes the record and does not prescribe a single required combination.

    本轮问题Discussion prompt

防遗漏：围绕证据提问Prevent omissions by asking about evidence

  • 开放性骨盆伤同时考虑出血、污染、软组织和脏器损伤，避免只盯骨折。Consider bleeding, contamination, soft tissue and organ injury in an open pelvic injury, avoiding a fracture-only focus.
  • 检查报告、术中发现和术后引流信息持续合并；明确“不可靠”和“未检查”。Reconcile investigation, operative and later drainage findings; mark unreliable and unexamined assessments.
  • 这是一组预防遗漏的教学问题；材料不足以证明原病例实际发生漏诊。These are missed-injury prevention questions; the source does not establish that an actual missed injury occurred.
展开教学说明Teaching detail

材料记载早期 CT 检查，但未提供足够的检查前稳定程度、复苏反应与转运条件。本课不据此推断当时决策正确或错误，也不推广“休克患者先做 CT”。课堂应向原救治团队核对当时依据。The source records early CT but does not sufficiently establish pre-scan stability, resuscitation response or transfer conditions. The lesson neither judges that decision nor generalizes CT-first care in shock. The original team should clarify the context.

本土证据：从队列回到 MDT 问题Local evidence: from cohort to MDT questions

Ding 等，IJEM 2025：32 例多发伤合并骨盆骨折的单中心回顾性研究；报告死亡 3/32（9.4%），胸部合并伤 28/32（87.5%）。Ding et al., IJEM 2025: a single-center retrospective cohort of 32 polytrauma patients with pelvic fractures, reporting 3/32 deaths (9.4%) and 28/32 associated thoracic injuries (87.5%).

教学启示：同时看全身损伤、生理趋势和跨专科接续。方法报告单因素及相关性分析；不能据此证明 MDT 降低死亡，或把 GCS/ISS 解释为已验证的独立因果因素。Teaching implication: integrate whole-body injury, physiological trends and specialty transitions.
