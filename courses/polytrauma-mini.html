主诉Presenting complaint
高速碰撞后多部位疼痛，伴呼吸和意识异常。Multisite pain after high-speed collision with abnormal breathing and consciousness.
现病史History
高速机动车碰撞，被困20分钟；头胸腹骨盆均可能受力。确切伤后时间、病史、抗凝及过敏待核实。High-speed collision with 20-minute entrapment; possible head, chest, abdominal and pelvic injury. Verify injury time, history, anticoagulants and allergies.
院前交接Prehospital handoff
核对脱困过程、颈椎保护、止血与固定、氧疗、用药和血液/补液记录；原病例未给出具体措施，不能虚构已做。Verify extrication, spinal precautions, bleeding control, splinting, oxygen, drugs and fluids/blood; specific prehospital actions were not supplied.

01

解剖与生理并列Keep anatomy and physiology together

损伤名称不等于当前休克原因；结合机制、连续灌注、全身出血来源和复苏反应更新风险。An injury label does not establish the current cause of shock. Update risk using mechanism, serial perfusion, all bleeding sources and response to resuscitation.

02

优先级可并行Priorities can run in parallel

出血控制、复苏、保温、凝血评估和资源启动相互衔接。不稳定且无反应的患者不应因 CT 延误必要的出血控制。Bleeding control, resuscitation, warming, coagulation assessment and resource activation are coordinated. CT should not delay necessary haemorrhage control in an unstable nonresponder.

03

颅脑损伤是重要例外Brain injury changes assumptions

不能把无脑损伤条件下的允许性低血压策略套用到重型 TBI。意识下降同时核对低氧、休克和药物等混杂因素，避免由 GCS 单独归因。Do not apply permissive hypotension intended for patients without brain injury to severe TBI. Assess hypoxia, shock and drug confounders when mentation falls; GCS alone does not establish cause.

暂停判断：束带后影像改善，能宣布出血停止吗？Pause and decide: Imaging improves with a binder. Can bleeding be declared controlled?

不能。复位形态和止血效果分别判断，继续追踪灌注、凝血和资源路径；束带移除或进一步影像由团队规划。No. Reduction and haemostasis are separate assessments. Continue perfusion/coagulation review and resource coordination; binder removal and further imaging require a team plan.

床旁救治与专科接续Bedside care and specialist continuity

每项措施都要回答：为什么做、由谁做、做后看什么。侵入操作由受训获授权人员执行，学员在监督下训练。For every action: why, who and what to reassess. Trained authorised operators perform invasive care; learners practise under supervision.

01

床旁：多条救命任务并行Bedside: parallel life-saving tasks

外出血控制、气道/氧合、胸部可逆威胁、出血复苏、保暖与监护同步分工；每项行动后回报生理反应。Assign bleeding control, airway/oxygenation, reversible chest threats, haemorrhage resuscitation, warming and monitoring in parallel; report physiologic response after each action.

02

止血与影像：按生理选择Haemostasis / imaging: choose by physiology

持续失稳优先可实施的止血路径；能安全完成才讨论全身CT。伴脑损伤不能直接套用允许性低血压。Persistent instability prioritises feasible haemostasis; discuss whole-body CT when safe. Do not apply permissive hypotension mechanically with brain injury.

03

多专科/重症：接续与遗漏伤Specialists / ICU: continuity and missed injuries

持续追踪灌注、凝血、体温与钙；专科协商源控制，稳定后三级查体、遗漏伤筛查和康复。Track perfusion, haemostasis, temperature and calcium; coordinate source control, then tertiary survey, missed injuries and rehabilitation after stabilisation.

ATLS 11 · 创伤团队框架ATLS 11 · trauma-team frame
教材式核心Textbook core多发伤不是多个单病种的简单相加。胸部损伤会改变氧合和通气，失血会改变脑与肾灌注，颅脑损伤又会改变循环目标和查体可靠性；镇痛、镇静、搬运和影像等待也会改变观察结果。每项新信息都必须回到整体生理重新排序。Polytrauma is not a sum of isolated diagnoses. Chest injury changes oxygenation and ventilation; bleeding changes cerebral and renal perfusion; TBI changes circulatory goals and examination reliability; analgesia, sedation, transport, and imaging delays alter observations. Every new datum must return to whole-patient physiology and reprioritization.
三张同步地图Three synchronized maps

患者地图：xABCDE 与趋势；损伤地图：头胸腹骨盆脊柱四肢；资源地图：手术、介入、影像、血液、重症和转运状态。三图时间戳必须一致。Patient map: xABCDE and trends; injury map: head, chest, abdomen, pelvis, spine, limbs; resource map: operating, interventional, imaging, blood, critical care, and transfer status. Keep all three time-stamped.

并行而非混乱Parallel, not chaotic

每项任务写清“目的—责任人—预计更新时间—失败后的升级路径”。团队负责人保留全局视角，避免所有人围绕一个检查或一个部位工作。Every task states purpose, owner, expected update, and escalation if it fails. The team leader preserves the global view so the whole team does not cluster around one test or body region.

复评触发Reassessment triggers

生命体征变化、新查体、转运前后、镇静/镇痛后、操作后和新影像报告都触发一次简短全身复评，并重写当前主导风险。Vital-sign change, a new finding, transport, analgesia/sedation, procedures, and new imaging each trigger a concise whole-body reassessment and a rewritten dominant risk.

生理优先Physiology first

识别可立即致命并可逆的气道、呼吸、出血和循环问题；避免因等待完整诊断而延误团队支持。Identify immediately lethal and reversible airway, breathing, hemorrhage, and circulatory problems; do not delay team support while waiting for complete diagnosis.

止血与凝血Hemorrhage and coagulation

出血控制、保温、连续凝血评估和目标导向复苏属于同一条路径；具体血液制品与药物由机构大出血流程决定。Hemorrhage control, warming, repeated coagulation assessment, and goal-directed resuscitation belong on one pathway; products and medications follow the institutional major-hemorrhage protocol.

冲突协调Conflict coordination

当颅脑、胸部和出血目标冲突时，明确哪个风险当前主导、哪个可并行处理、何时重新讨论，并邀请相应专科共同决策。When TBI, thoracic, and hemorrhage goals conflict, name the dominant risk, parallelizable work, time for renewed discussion, and specialties needed for joint decisions.
