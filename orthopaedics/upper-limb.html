1肩关节脱位：单纯脱位 vs 骨折脱位Shoulder Dislocation: Simple vs Fracture-Dislocation

1.1　解剖与机制1.1　Anatomy & Mechanism

  • 盂肱关节是典型的球窝关节，活动度大，稳定性主要依赖软组织：盂唇、关节囊、肩袖与韧带。The glenohumeral joint is a classic ball-and-socket joint with a large range of motion; stability relies mainly on soft tissues — labrum, capsule, rotator cuff and ligaments.
  • 前脱位最常见。典型机制：上肢外展外旋位受暴力，或跌倒时手撑地。Anterior dislocation is the most common. Typical mechanism: force applied with the arm abducted and externally rotated, or a fall onto the outstretched hand.
  • 后脱位少见，多见于电击伤、癫痫发作或高能量创伤，容易漏诊。Posterior dislocation is uncommon, seen after electric shock, seizures or high-energy trauma, and is easily missed.
  • 单纯脱位：仅关节脱位，无骨折。骨折脱位：脱位合并肱骨近端骨折，最常见为大结节骨折。Simple dislocation: dislocation without fracture. Fracture-dislocation: dislocation with a proximal humerus fracture, most commonly of the greater tuberosity.
展开：为什么前脱位最常见？Expand: why is anterior dislocation the most common?
  • 关节盂浅、前下方骨性约束最弱，外展外旋时肱骨头容易向前下方滑出。The glenoid is shallow and bony constraint is weakest anteroinferiorly; in abduction-external rotation the humeral head slides out anteroinferiorly.
  • 前脱位常合并 Bankart 损伤（前下盂唇撕脱）与 Hill-Sachs 损伤（肱骨头后外侧压缩骨折），是复发性不稳定的解剖基础。Anterior dislocation is often accompanied by a Bankart lesion (anteroinferior labral detachment) and a Hill-Sachs lesion (posterolateral humeral head compression fracture) — the anatomic basis of recurrent instability.

1.2　初评和红旗1.2　Initial Assessment & Red Flags

  • 先看全身：多发伤患者按 ATLS 流程，肩部不是优先处理部位。Check the whole patient first: in polytrauma follow ATLS; the shoulder is not the first priority.
  • 视诊：方肩畸形、患肢轻度外展弹性固定。Inspection: squared-off shoulder contour, arm held in slight abduction with elastic resistance.
  • 神经血管检查必须在复位前、后各做一次并记录。Neurovascular examination is mandatory before and after reduction, and must be documented.
红旗Red flags
  • 腋神经损伤：三角肌区（上臂外侧）感觉减退、主动外展无力。Axillary nerve injury: reduced sensation over the deltoid region (lateral upper arm), weak active abduction.
  • 血管损伤体征：桡动脉搏动减弱/消失、手部苍白发凉（少见但危重）。Vascular injury signs: diminished/absent radial pulse, pale cool hand (rare but critical).
  • 皮肤破损/开放性脱位：按开放性损伤流程处理。Skin breach / open dislocation: manage as an open injury.
  • 复位后仍有神经症状或血管体征：立即上级评估。Persistent neurologic symptoms or vascular signs after reduction: escalate immediately.

1.3　影像 / 分类1.3　Imaging / Classification

  • 常规：肩关节正位 + 腋位（或 Y 位），确认脱位方向、排除骨折。Routine: AP + axillary (or Y) views to confirm direction and exclude fracture.
  • 怀疑骨折脱位、骨缺损或复位困难：加做 CT（含三维重建）。If fracture-dislocation, bony defect or difficult reduction is suspected: add CT (with 3D reconstruction).
  • 复位后必须复查 X 线，确认肱骨头同心圆复位。Post-reduction radiographs are mandatory to confirm concentric reduction of the humeral head.
展开：分类（方向 × 是否合并骨折）Expand: classification (direction × fracture or not)
  • 按方向：前脱位、后脱位、下脱位（盂下）、上脱位（罕见）。By direction: anterior, posterior, inferior (luxatio erecta), superior (rare).
  • 按是否合并骨折：单纯脱位 vs 骨折脱位（大结节 / 外科颈 / 解剖颈 / 头部劈裂）。By associated fracture: simple dislocation vs fracture-dislocation (greater tuberosity / surgical neck / anatomic neck / head-split).
  • 分类用于描述损伤，不等于手术指征。是否手术取决于骨折移位、关节稳定性与患者因素。Classification describes the injury — it is not an indication for surgery. Operative decisions depend on fracture displacement, joint stability and patient factors.

1.4　AO 原则如何适用1.4　How AO Principles Apply

  • AO 四原则：解剖复位、稳定固定、保护血供、早期功能锻炼——主要针对骨折；且“解剖复位”按部位理解：关节面追求解剖复位，骨干恢复长度、对线与旋转即可。The four AO principles — anatomic reduction, stable fixation, preservation of blood supply, early functional exercise — apply primarily to fractures; and "anatomic reduction" is site-specific: articular surfaces pursue anatomic reduction, while diaphyseal fractures need length, alignment and rotation restored.
  • 稳定性与之匹配：关节内骨折多需绝对稳定，骨干骨折相对稳定下的二期愈合同样是 AO 认可的路径。Match stability to the site: intra-articular fractures usually need absolute stability; diaphyseal fractures heal well by secondary intention under relative stability — equally an AO-recognized pathway.
  • 单纯脱位：核心是恢复关节同心圆对合（复位），然后通过短期制动 + 渐进康复恢复功能。Simple dislocation: the core is restoring concentric joint congruity (reduction), then short immobilisation plus progressive rehabilitation.
  • 骨折脱位：按骨折处理——先评估骨折块，再决定复位顺序与固定方式；保护肱骨头血供是关键考量。Fracture-dislocation: manage as a fracture — assess fragments first, then decide the reduction sequence and fixation; protecting humeral head blood supply is a key consideration.
  • 早期功能锻炼适用于稳定复位之后；不稳定的关节过早活动会导致再脱位。Early mobilisation applies after a stable reduction; moving an unstable joint too early risks re-dislocation.

1.5　治疗选择与限制1.5　Treatment Options & Limits

单纯脱位Simple闭合复位为主Closed reduction first

  • 在充分镇静镇痛下轻柔复位；忌暴力。Reduce gently under adequate sedation and analgesia; never use force.
  • 复位后：复查神经血管 + X 线；悬吊短期制动，制动时长个体化。After reduction: recheck neurovascular status + radiographs; sling immobilisation for a short, individualised period.
  • 年轻、运动需求高的首发前脱位患者复发风险高，需康复随访与复发预案。Young, active patients with a first-time anterior dislocation carry a high recurrence risk — arrange rehabilitation follow-up and a recurrence plan.

骨折脱位Fracture-dislocation按骨折原则处理Managed by fracture principles

  • 先明确骨折块大小、移位与关节面受累（CT 与否依据骨折复杂度和临床稳定性决定），再定方案；若合并血管损伤等危及肢体的情况，紧急处置优先，不因等待影像而延误。First define fragment size, displacement and articular involvement (CT as fracture complexity and clinical stability dictate), then decide; with vascular injury or other limb-threatening conditions, urgent management takes priority and must not wait for imaging.
  • 大结节明显移位、关节面劈裂或复位后不稳定：多需手术（固定或按肱骨近端骨折处理）。Markedly displaced greater tuberosity, articular head-split or post-reduction instability: usually operative (fixation, managed as a proximal humerus fracture).
  • 无明显移位的骨折脱位：可在麻醉下复位后按肱骨近端骨折保守治疗并密切随访。Minimally displaced fracture-dislocation: may be reduced under anaesthesia and managed non-operatively as a proximal humerus fracture with close follow-up.
展开：治疗的限制与注意事项Expand: limits and cautions
  • 本课程不规定具体复位手法与药物剂量；手法选择与镇静方案遵循本院流程与麻醉科协作。This course does not prescribe specific reduction manoeuvres or drug doses; follow local protocols with anaesthesia support.
  • 骨质疏松患者：骨折脱位风险更高，固定选择需考虑骨质量。Osteoporotic patients: higher fracture-dislocation risk; fixation choices must account for bone quality.
  • 陈旧性脱位（数周未复位）：不要强行闭合复位，需专科评估。Chronic dislocation (unreduced for weeks): do not force closed reduction — refer for specialist assessment.

1.6　并发症 / 随访1.6　Complications / Follow-up

  • 复发性脱位 / 慢性不稳定：尤其年轻首发前脱位患者。Recurrent dislocation / chronic instability: especially young patients after a first anterior dislocation.
  • 骨缺损相关不稳：Bankart 与 Hill-Sachs 病变进展。Bony-defect-related instability: progression of Bankart and Hill-Sachs lesions.
  • 腋神经损伤：多数为牵拉伤可恢复，需随访肌力与感觉。Axillary nerve injury: usually a traction injury that recovers; follow strength and sensation.
  • 创伤性关节炎：反复脱位或关节面损伤后远期风险。Post-traumatic arthritis: long-term risk after recurrent dislocation or articular injury.
  • 随访要点：康复计划（活动度→力量→本体感觉）、重返运动/劳动的个体化标准。Follow-up: rehabilitation (motion → strength → proprioception), individualised return-to-sport/work criteria.

步骤 1 · 病史与查体Step 1 · History & exam
方肩畸形，患肢弹性固定于轻度外展位。桡动脉搏动可及，手部温暖。三角肌区感觉减退，主动外展不能（疼痛限制 vs 神经损伤，待鉴别）。Squared-off shoulder; arm elastically fixed in slight abduction. Radial pulse palpable, hand warm. Reduced sensation over the deltoid; active abduction impossible (pain vs nerve injury — to be clarified).
步骤 2 · 影像Step 2 · Imaging

步骤 3 · 处理Step 3 · Management
充分镇痛镇静下轻柔闭合复位。复位后：方肩畸形消失，复查 X 线示同心圆复位；再查神经血管并记录——三角肌区感觉仍减退，拟诊腋神经牵拉伤，安排随访。Gentle closed reduction under adequate sedation/analgesia. After reduction: deformity resolved, radiographs confirm concentric reduction; neurovascular recheck documented — deltoid numbness persists, presumed axillary nerve traction injury, follow-up arranged.
步骤 4 · 后续Step 4 · Follow-up
悬吊短期制动后转康复：活动度→力量→本体感觉。告知年轻首发前脱位复发风险高，若再脱位或出现交锁感及时复诊。Short sling immobilisation then rehabilitation: motion → strength → proprioception. Counsel that first-time anterior dislocation in a young patient carries high recurrence risk; return promptly if it re-dislocates or locking occurs.

Q1. 肩关节前脱位复位后，最关键的即刻复查是什么？Q1. What is the most important immediate recheck after reducing an anterior shoulder dislocation?

答案：CAnswer: C
复位前后都必须做神经血管检查并记录，X 线确认肱骨头真正复位（排除骨折脱位漏诊）。过早全范围活动（A）可致再脱位；D 无指征。Neurovascular status must be checked and documented before and after reduction, and radiographs must confirm true concentric reduction (excluding a missed fracture-dislocation). Q2. 同一患者 X 线若同时显示大结节骨折块明显移位，处理策略应如何调整？Q2. If the same patient's radiograph also shows a markedly displaced greater tuberosity fragment, how should management change?

2.1　解剖与机制2.1　Anatomy & Mechanism

  • 肘关节由肱尺、肱桡、上尺桡三个关节组成，稳定性依赖骨性结构（冠状突、鹰嘴）与内外侧副韧带。The elbow comprises the ulnohumeral, radiocapitellar and proximal radioulnar joints; stability depends on bony structures (coronoid, olecranon) and the collateral ligaments.
  • 后脱位最常见，典型机制是跌倒时手撑地、肘过伸伴旋转暴力。Posterior dislocation is the most common; typical mechanism is a fall onto the outstretched hand with hyperextension and rotational force.
  • 单纯脱位：无骨折。骨折脱位（复杂脱位）：合并桡骨头 / 冠状突 / 鹰嘴等骨折。Simple dislocation: no fracture. Fracture-dislocation (complex dislocation): with fractures of the radial head, coronoid, olecranon, etc.
  • 恐怖三联征 = 肘关节后脱位 + 桡骨头骨折 + 冠状突骨折，常合并外侧副韧带损伤，高度不稳定。The terrible triad = posterior elbow dislocation + radial head fracture + coronoid fracture, often with lateral collateral ligament injury — highly unstable.
展开：为什么冠状突如此重要？Expand: why is the coronoid so important?
  • 冠状突是肘关节前方的骨性阻挡，缺损越大，肘关节越容易向后半脱位。The coronoid is the anterior bony buttress of the elbow; the larger the defect, the more prone the elbow is to posterior subluxation.
  • 因此骨折脱位中冠状突骨折块的大小与移位，是判断稳定性的核心依据之一。Hence, in fracture-dislocation, the size and displacement of the coronoid fragment are core determinants of stability.

2.2　初评和红旗2.2　Initial Assessment & Red Flags

  • 肘部畸形常很明显，但仍需按流程完成全身评估。Elbow deformity is often obvious, but the full systematic assessment still comes first.
  • 复位前、后各做一次神经血管检查并记录：桡动脉搏动、正中/尺/桡神经功能。Perform and document a neurovascular examination before and after reduction: radial pulse and median/ulnar/radial nerve function.
红旗Red flags
  • 肱动脉损伤：尤其合并肱骨远端/近端骨折时；搏动减弱、手部苍白发凉。Brachial artery injury: especially with associated distal/proximal humeral fractures; weak pulse, pale cool hand.
  • 骨筋膜室综合征：前臂剧痛、被动牵拉痛、张力性肿胀。Compartment syndrome: severe forearm pain, pain on passive stretch, tense swelling.
  • 开放性脱位 / 皮肤破损：按开放性损伤处理。Open dislocation / skin breach: manage as an open injury.
  • 复位后仍不稳定、反复半脱位：提示关键稳定结构损伤，需进一步评估。Persistent instability or recurrent subluxation after reduction: suggests injury to key stabilisers — further evaluation needed.

2.3　影像 / 分类2.3　Imaging / Classification

  • 常规：肘关节正侧位 X 线，判断脱位方向、有无骨折。Routine: AP and lateral elbow radiographs to determine direction and detect fractures.
  • 怀疑骨折脱位：CT 明确桡骨头、冠状突骨折块的大小与移位。Suspected fracture-dislocation: CT to define the size and displacement of radial head and coronoid fragments.
  • 复位后复查 X 线，确认关节同心圆复位、无残留半脱位。Post-reduction radiographs to confirm concentric reduction without residual subluxation.
展开：分类Expand: classification
  • 按方向：后脱位（最常见）、前脱位、内/外侧脱位。By direction: posterior (most common), anterior, medial/lateral.
  • 按是否合并骨折：单纯脱位 vs 骨折脱位（含恐怖三联征）。By associated fracture: simple dislocation vs fracture-dislocation (including the terrible triad).
  • 分类描述损伤，不等于手术指征；是否手术看复位后稳定性与骨折情况。Classification describes the injury, not the indication for surgery; operative decisions rest on post-reduction stability and the fracture pattern.

2.4　AO 原则如何适用2.4　How AO Principles Apply

  • 肘关节的核心目标是：稳定 + 同心圆复位 + 早期活动。The core goals at the elbow are: stability + concentric reduction + early motion.
  • 解剖复位：骨折块（冠状突、桡骨头）复位到解剖位置，恢复骨性稳定。Anatomic reduction: restore fracture fragments (coronoid, radial head) to reconstruct bony stability.
  • 稳定固定：使关节在早期活动中保持同心圆对合，避免长期制动导致的僵硬。Stable fixation: keeps the joint concentrically reduced during early motion, avoiding stiffness from prolonged immobilisation.
  • 保护血供与软组织：骨折脱位周围软组织损伤重，手术入路需保护重要结构。Preserve blood supply and soft tissues: soft-tissue injury is severe in fracture-dislocation; approaches must protect key structures.

2.5　治疗选择与限制2.5　Treatment Options & Limits

单纯脱位·稳定Simple · stable复位后早期活动Early motion after reduction

  • 肘关节脱位属急症：尽快接受专业评估，在安全条件下复位（充分镇痛镇静、轻柔手法），复位前后检查神经血管与稳定性。Elbow dislocation is an emergency: seek prompt professional assessment and reduce under safe conditions (adequate sedation/analgesia, gentle manoeuvre), checking neurovascular status and stability before and after reduction.
  • 复位后稳定：短期支具保护下尽早开始主动活动，避免长期制动。If stable after reduction: begin active motion as early as possible with short-term brace protection; avoid prolonged immobilisation.

骨折脱位Fracture-dislocation按损伤逐一处理，不套用同一方案Address each injury; no one-size-fits-all

  • 桡骨头骨折：小块稳定可保守；移位明显或影响稳定 → 固定或置换（按骨折块大小与粉碎程度个体化）。Radial head fracture: small stable fragments may be non-operative; displaced or stability-compromising fractures → fixation or replacement, individualised to fragment size and comminution.
  • 冠状突骨折：大块移位影响稳定 → 固定恢复前方骨性阻挡。Coronoid fracture: large displaced fragments compromising stability → fixation to restore the anterior buttress.
  • 韧带损伤：外侧副韧带撕裂常需修复，以恢复旋转稳定性。Ligament injury: lateral collateral ligament tears often need repair to restore rotatory stability.
  • 恐怖三联征：三个稳定结构均受损，多数需手术系统重建。Terrible triad: all three stabilisers injured; most need systematic operative reconstruction.
展开：限制与注意事项Expand: limits and cautions
  • 肘关节最怕僵硬：能早期活动就不长期制动，能稳定固定就不靠石膏硬扛。The elbow's greatest enemy is stiffness: mobilise early when possible; don't rely on prolonged casting when stable fixation is achievable.
  • 复位困难或复位后反复不稳：不要反复暴力尝试，及时请专科会诊。Difficult reduction or recurrent instability: don't repeat forceful attempts — get specialist help.
  • 本课程不规定具体手术入路与内固定型号选择，遵循 AO Surgery Reference 与本院流程。This course does not prescribe specific approaches or implant choices; follow AO Surgery Reference and local protocols.

2.6　并发症 / 随访2.6　Complications / Follow-up

  • 关节僵硬：最常见，康复是治疗的一部分。Joint stiffness: the most common; rehabilitation is part of treatment.
  • 残留不稳定 / 再脱位：提示稳定结构未充分恢复。Residual instability / re-dislocation: suggests incompletely restored stabilisers.
  • 异位骨化：高能量损伤与手术后可能出现，影响活动度。Heterotopic ossification: may follow high-energy injury or surgery and limit motion.
  • 尺神经症状、创伤性关节炎：中远期随访观察。Ulnar nerve symptoms, post-traumatic arthritis: watch for in mid- to long-term follow-up.
  • 随访要点：活动度进展、稳定性复查、神经症状变化。Follow-up: progress of motion, stability recheck, changes in neurologic symptoms.

45 岁女性，跌倒时右手撑地，右肘剧痛畸形。逐步揭晓。A 45-year-old woman fell onto her right outstretched hand; the right elbow is severely painful and deformed. Reveal step by step.

步骤 1 · 查体Step 1 · Exam
肘后空虚、鹰嘴向后突出。桡动脉搏动可及，前臂无张力性肿胀，被动牵拉手指疼痛不明显。正中/尺/桡神经初查无缺失。Hollow posterior elbow, olecranon prominent posteriorly. Radial pulse palpable; no tense forearm swelling; no marked pain on passive finger stretch. Median/ulnar/radial nerves grossly intact.
步骤 2 · 影像Step 2 · Imaging
X 线：肘关节后脱位，桡骨头可见骨折线，冠状突小骨折块。CT 确认：桡骨头骨折轻度移位、未造成前臂旋转的机械性阻挡；冠状突尖部小骨折（Regan–Morrey Ⅰ/Ⅱ型范围）。以下数字仅描述本例，不构成自动阈值。Radiographs: posterior elbow dislocation with a radial head fracture line and a small coronoid fragment. The numbers here describe this case only and are not automatic thresholds.
步骤 3 · 决策Step 3 · Decision
麻醉下闭合复位成功：透视下肘关节同心圆复位，前臂旋转无机械性阻挡，在可接受的活动弧内保持稳定。本例在同时满足严格筛选条件后选择保守（依据 Chan 等 2014 小样本病例系列，见本单元来源 4）：同心圆复位、无机械性旋转阻挡、冠状突骨折小、有稳定活动弧、能做到密切的临床与影像随访。方案：短期支具 + 早期保护下活动，密切随访稳定性与骨折愈合。Successful closed reduction under anaesthesia: fluoroscopy shows concentric reduction, no mechanical block to forearm rotation, and stability within an acceptable arc of motion. This case met strict selection criteria before non-operative management was chosen (per the small case series by Chan et al. 2014, Unit 2 source 4): concentric reduction, no mechanical block to rotation, small coronoid fracture, a stable arc of motion, and capacity for close clinical and radiographic follow-up. Plan: short-term bracing + early protected motion, with close follow-up of stability and healing.
步骤 4 · 警示Step 4 · Caveat
注意：这是一个严格筛选后的例外——多数恐怖三联征仍需手术系统重建（桡骨头、冠状突、外侧副韧带逐一处理）。该保守路径的证据为回顾性小系列（12 例，Level IV），外推需谨慎；切勿把“可保守”当成常规，更不能只凭“骨折面积 1/3、伸直 30°”两个数字自动判定。Note: this is a strictly selected exception — most terrible triads still need systematic operative reconstruction (radial head, coronoid, LCL addressed one by one). 3肱骨骨折：近端 / 骨干 / 远端Humeral Fractures: Proximal / Shaft / Distal

本单元导读Unit guide
近端、骨干、远端是三个不同的解剖部位，治疗逻辑完全不同：近端多可保守、骨干警惕桡神经、远端关节内骨折多需手术。本单元三个部位分开论述，不套用同一方案。Proximal, shaft and distal are three different anatomic regions with entirely different logic: proximal fractures are often non-operative, shaft fractures demand vigilance for the radial nerve, and distal intra-articular fractures often need surgery. The three are discussed separately — no single protocol.

3.1　解剖与机制3.1　Anatomy & Mechanism

近端Proximal四个部分：肱骨头、大结节、小结节、肱骨干Four segments: head, greater tuberosity, lesser tuberosity, shaft

  • 肱骨头由旋肱前、后动脉及其吻合网共同供血——一项尸体定量研究发现后旋肱动脉的贡献大于既往认识（见来源 4；该比例为该研究的测量结果，不外推为所有患者的固定数值），手术入路与固定时注意保护骨折块的软组织附着；骨折移位越重，缺血坏死风险越高。The humeral head is supplied jointly by the anterior and posterior circumflex humeral arteries and their anastomoses — a quantitative cadaveric study found the posterior vessel contributes more than previously thought (source 4; that study's proportions are its own measurements, not fixed values for all patients); protect the fragments' soft-tissue attachments during approach and fixation. Greater displacement raises avascular necrosis risk.
  • 老年人骨质疏松性跌倒是主要机制；年轻人多为高能量创伤。Osteoporotic falls dominate in the elderly; high-energy trauma in the young.

骨干Shaft桡神经在中段螺旋沟紧贴骨面Radial nerve hugs the bone in the spiral groove

  • 桡神经在肱骨中段后方螺旋沟内走行，骨折极易牵拉或嵌顿。The radial nerve runs in the spiral groove posteriorly at the midshaft — easily stretched or entrapped by fracture.
  • 远端 1/3 螺旋形骨折（Holstein-Lewis）桡神经嵌顿风险更高。Distal-third spiral fractures (Holstein-Lewis) carry higher risk of radial nerve entrapment.

远端Distal内/外侧柱 + 滑车/小头关节面Medial/lateral columns + trochlea/capitellum

  • 远端呈三角形柱状结构，关节面包括滑车（对尺骨）与小头（对桡骨）。The distal humerus is a triangular columnar structure; the articular surface comprises the trochlea (ulna) and capitellum (radius).
  • 尺神经在内上髁后方尺神经沟内走行，骨折与手术均需保护。The ulnar nerve runs in the groove behind the medial epicondyle — protect it in both fracture and surgery.

3.2　初评和红旗3.2　Initial Assessment & Red Flags

  • 视诊触诊：畸形、肿胀、压痛部位定位骨折节段；检查皮肤完整性。Inspection and palpation: deformity, swelling and point tenderness localise the segment; check skin integrity.
  • 三个部位各有必须查的神经：近端—腋神经；骨干—桡神经；远端—尺神经（兼查正中/桡神经）。Each region has its must-check nerve: proximal — axillary; shaft — radial; distal — ulnar (plus median/radial).
  • 血管：触桡动脉搏动；高能量损伤警惕血管损伤。Vascular: palpate the radial pulse; remain vigilant in high-energy injuries.
红旗Red flags
  • 垂腕 / 虎口区麻木：桡神经损伤（骨干骨折最常见）。Wrist drop / dorsal hand numbness: radial nerve injury (most common with shaft fractures).
  • 手部小指侧麻木、夹纸无力：尺神经症状（远端骨折）。Ulnar-sided hand numbness, weak finger adduction: ulnar nerve symptoms (distal fractures).
  • 进行性加重的剧痛、被动牵拉痛：警惕骨筋膜室综合征。Progressively worsening severe pain, pain on passive stretch: suspect compartment syndrome.
  • 开放性骨折：按开放性骨折流程（清创、抗生素、破伤风）处理。Open fracture: manage per open-fracture protocol (debridement, antibiotics, tetanus).

3.3　影像 / 分类3.3　Imaging / Classification

  • 常规：肱骨正侧位 X 线（含上下关节）。Routine: AP and lateral humerus radiographs (including adjacent joints).
  • 近端复杂骨折：CT 明确节段移位与关节面情况。Complex proximal fractures: CT to define segment displacement and articular involvement.
  • 远端关节内骨折：CT（含三维）明确柱与关节面粉碎程度。Distal intra-articular fractures: CT (with 3D) to define column and articular comminution.
展开：分类概念（不等于手术指征）Expand: classification concepts (not operative indications)
  • 近端：Neer 四部分概念——按移位的解剖节段（头、大结节、小结节、干）计数。Proximal: Neer's four-segment concept — counting displaced anatomic segments (head, greater/lesser tuberosity, shaft).
  • 骨干：按骨折线形态（横形、斜形、螺旋形、粉碎）与部位（上/中/下 1/3）描述。Shaft: described by fracture-line pattern (transverse, oblique, spiral, comminuted) and location (proximal/middle/distal third).
  • 远端：按是否累及关节面、柱的完整性描述（部分关节内 / 完全关节内）。Distal: described by articular involvement and column integrity (partial- vs complete-articular).
  • 以上分类用于沟通与记录；是否手术取决于移位程度、稳定性、患者年龄功能需求与骨质量。These classifications aid communication and documentation; operative decisions depend on displacement, stability, the patient's age and functional demands, and bone quality.

3.4　AO 原则如何适用3.4　How AO Principles Apply

  • 近端：血供保护优先——肱骨头缺血坏死是主要顾虑，粉碎严重时固定与血供之间需权衡。Proximal: blood-supply preservation first — humeral head avascular necrosis is the main concern; weigh fixation against perfusion in severe comminution.
  • 骨干：根据骨折模式、软组织条件、伴随损伤与患者功能需求，在功能支具保守与手术之间选择；两者都是合理选项，关键是与适应证匹配。Shaft: choose between functional bracing and surgery based on fracture pattern, soft-tissue condition, associated injuries and the patient's functional demands; both are legitimate options — matching the indication is what matters.
  • 远端（成人关节内）：解剖复位 + 双柱稳定固定 + 早期活动，三者缺一不可。Distal (adult intra-articular): anatomic reduction + bicolumnar stable fixation + early motion — all three are essential.

3.5　治疗选择与限制3.5　Treatment Options & Limits

近端Proximal多数可保守，手术看移位与患者Mostly non-operative; surgery depends on displacement & patient

  • 无移位 / 轻度移位：悬吊制动后渐进康复，多数愈合良好。Non-displaced / minimally displaced: sling then progressive rehabilitation; most heal well.
  • 移位明显的两部分、三部分骨折：年轻、骨质量好 → 多考虑切开复位内固定。Displaced two- or three-part fractures: young patients with good bone → usually open reduction and internal fixation.
  • 高龄、粉碎、骨质量差的四部分骨折：固定困难时可考虑关节置换，个体化决策。Elderly patients with comminuted four-part fractures and poor bone: arthroplasty may be considered when fixation is unfeasible — individualised.

骨干Shaft按骨折与患者情况选择支具或手术，警惕桡神经Brace or surgery by fracture and patient factors; watch the radial nerve

  • 功能支具 / 悬吊 + 早期肩肘活动：适用于骨折模式稳定、软组织条件好、无伴随损伤需手术者。Functional bracing / sling + early shoulder-elbow motion: for stable fracture patterns, good soft-tissue conditions and no associated injuries requiring surgery.
  • 原发性桡神经损伤：多为牵拉伤，观察随访为主，记录基线功能。Primary radial nerve injury: usually a traction injury — observe with follow-up; document baseline function.
  • 手术指征（示例非穷尽）：开放骨折、血管损伤需修复、浮肘、多发伤需早期活动、复位后无法维持可接受对线。Operative indications (not exhaustive): open fracture, vascular injury needing repair, floating elbow, polytrauma needing early mobilisation, unacceptable alignment that cannot be maintained.
  • Holstein-Lewis 骨折合并桡神经损伤：警惕神经嵌顿，需专科评估。Holstein-Lewis fracture with radial nerve injury: beware entrapment — specialist assessment needed.

远端Distal成人关节内多需手术Adult intra-articular often operative

  • 关节外 / 轻度移位：可保守，但需保证早期活动防僵硬。Extra-articular / minimally displaced: may be non-operative, but ensure early motion to prevent stiffness.
  • 成人关节内移位骨折：解剖复位 + 内外侧柱稳定固定，术后早期活动。Displaced adult intra-articular fractures: anatomic reduction + stable medial-lateral column fixation, then early motion.
  • 术中保护尺神经；粉碎严重的高龄患者可个体化考虑置换选项。Protect the ulnar nerve intra-operatively; in severely comminuted elderly fractures, replacement may be individualised.

  • 三个部位不共用同一治疗方案，决策时回到各部位的解剖与功能目标。The three regions do not share one protocol; return to each region's anatomy and functional goals when deciding.

3.6　并发症 / 随访3.6　Complications / Follow-up

  • 近端：肱骨头缺血坏死、骨折不愈合/畸形愈合、肩关节僵硬。Proximal: humeral head avascular necrosis, nonunion/malunion, shoulder stiffness.
  • 骨干：桡神经损伤（观察恢复情况）、骨不连（警惕长期不愈合）。Shaft: radial nerve injury (monitor recovery), nonunion (watch for delayed healing).
  • 远端：肘关节僵硬（最常见）、尺神经症状、创伤性关节炎、内固定相关问题。Distal: elbow stiffness (most common), ulnar nerve symptoms, post-traumatic arthritis, implant-related issues.
  • 随访：X 线愈合进展、神经功能恢复、关节活动度；康复贯穿全程。Follow-up: radiographic healing, nerve recovery, range of motion; rehabilitation throughout.

4尺桡骨骨折：骨干 / 远端 · Monteggia / GaleazziForearm Fractures: Shaft / Distal · Monteggia / Galeazzi

本单元铁律Unit rule
每一例前臂骨折，都要看肘、看腕。Monteggia 漏看肘、Galeazzi 漏看腕，是前臂创伤最经典的漏诊。For every forearm fracture, examine the elbow and the wrist. Missing the elbow in Monteggia and the wrist in Galeazzi are the classic missed diagnoses of forearm trauma.

4.1　解剖与机制4.1　Anatomy & Mechanism

  • 尺桡骨通过上尺桡关节、骨间膜、下尺桡关节（DRUJ）连成一个旋转整体，前臂旋转功能依赖两骨的相对长度与对线。Radius and ulna form one rotational unit via the proximal radioulnar joint, interosseous membrane and DRUJ; forearm rotation depends on their relative length and alignment.
  • 双骨折（尺桡骨干双骨折）多为高能量直接暴力。Both-bone forearm fractures usually result from high-energy direct trauma.
  • Monteggia：尺骨近端骨折 + 桡骨头脱位（看肘）。Monteggia: proximal ulna fracture + radial head dislocation (check the elbow).
  • Galeazzi：桡骨干骨折 + 远端尺桡关节（DRUJ）脱位（看腕）。Galeazzi: radial shaft fracture + DRUJ dislocation (check the wrist).
  • 桡骨远端骨折：跌倒手撑地最常见；关注关节面、DRUJ 与腕管。Distal radius fracture: most commonly a fall onto the outstretched hand; assess the articular surface, DRUJ and carpal tunnel.
展开：为什么前臂双骨折多需手术？Expand: why do both-bone forearm fractures often need surgery?
  • 前臂旋转功能对两骨长度与成角极敏感，闭合复位难以同时维持两骨的解剖对线。Forearm rotation is highly sensitive to the length and angulation of both bones; closed reduction struggles to hold anatomic alignment of both.
  • 畸形愈合 → 旋转受限，这是前臂骨折区别于肱骨干骨折的关键。Malunion → loss of rotation. This is what sets forearm fractures apart from humeral shaft fractures.

4.2　初评和红旗4.2　Initial Assessment & Red Flags

  • 查骨折局部：畸形、肿胀、压痛、皮肤完整性。Examine the fracture site: deformity, swelling, tenderness, skin integrity.
  • 查肘：桡骨头有无脱位（Monteggia）。查腕：DRUJ 有无脱位、腕部压痛（Galeazzi）。Check the elbow: radial head dislocation (Monteggia). Check the wrist: DRUJ dislocation, wrist tenderness (Galeazzi).
  • 神经血管：桡动脉搏动；正中/尺/桡神经；骨间后神经（Monteggia 合并桡骨头脱位时）。Neurovascular: radial pulse; median/ulnar/radial nerves; posterior interosseous nerve (with Monteggia radial-head dislocation).
红旗Red flags
  • 急性腕管综合征：桡骨远端骨折后正中神经分布区进行性麻木、疼痛加剧。Acute carpal tunnel syndrome: progressive median-nerve numbness and worsening pain after distal radius fracture.
  • 骨筋膜室综合征：前臂张力性肿胀、被动牵拉痛。Compartment syndrome: tense forearm swelling, pain on passive stretch.
  • 开放性骨折 / 皮肤破损。Open fracture / skin breach.
  • 单骨骨折 X 线：必须追看肘与腕，否则 Monteggia / Galeazzi 漏诊。Single-bone fracture on X-ray: must review elbow and wrist, or Monteggia/Galeazzi will be missed.

4.3　影像 / 分类4.3　Imaging / Classification

  • 常规：前臂正侧位 X 线，必须包含肘关节与腕关节。Routine: AP and lateral forearm radiographs — must include the elbow and the wrist.
  • 桡骨远端：加腕关节正侧位，评估关节面台阶、DRUJ 对合。Distal radius: add wrist AP/lateral views to assess articular step and DRUJ congruity.
  • 复杂关节内骨折：CT 明确关节面粉碎与移位。Complex intra-articular fractures: CT to define articular comminution and displacement.
展开：分类概念Expand: classification concepts
  • 骨干：单骨 vs 双骨；骨折线形态与部位；是否合并关节脱位（Monteggia / Galeazzi 为特殊类型）。Shaft: single- vs both-bone; fracture-line pattern and location; associated joint dislocation (Monteggia/Galeazzi as special types).
  • 远端：关节外 vs 部分关节内 vs 完全关节内；DRUJ 是否受累。Distal: extra-articular vs partial-articular vs complete-articular; DRUJ involvement or not.
  • 分类用于描述，不等于手术指征。Classification describes; it is not an operative indication.

4.4　AO 原则如何适用4.4　How AO Principles Apply

  • 解剖复位在这里有功能含义：恢复两骨长度、对线与旋转对合，不只是“接上”。Anatomic reduction here has functional meaning: restoring length, alignment and rotational congruity of both bones — not just "joining" them.
  • 稳定固定：为早期功能锻炼创造条件；但若合并 DRUJ 损伤（如 Galeazzi），韧带愈合需优先保护，旋转锻炼须待稳定性确认后进行，不可过早自由旋转。Stable fixation: creates the conditions for early functional exercise; but with DRUJ injury (e.g. Galeazzi), ligament healing takes priority — rotational exercises only after stability is confirmed, never early free rotation.
  • 保护血供：前臂软组织包被少，手术入路注意保护皮神经与肌腱。Preserve blood supply: thin soft-tissue envelope — protect cutaneous nerves and tendons in surgical approaches.
  • Monteggia / Galeazzi：复位的核心是恢复脱位关节的对合，骨折固定为关节稳定服务。Monteggia/Galeazzi: the core of reduction is restoring the dislocated joint; fracture fixation serves joint stability.

4.5　治疗选择与限制4.5　Treatment Options & Limits

骨干双骨折Both-bone shaft成人多需手术恢复旋转功能Adults often need surgery to restore rotation

  • 成人尺桡骨干双骨折：多需切开复位内固定，恢复两骨解剖对线。Adult both-bone diaphyseal fractures: usually open reduction and internal fixation to restore anatomic alignment of both bones.
  • 术后在骨折稳定固定、且 DRUJ 无损伤的前提下，早期开始前臂旋转锻炼。After surgery, provided fixation is stable and the DRUJ is intact, begin forearm rotation exercises early.

Monteggia / GaleazziMonteggia / Galeazzi先认出，再按关节处理Recognise first, then treat the joint

  • Monteggia：复位固定尺骨骨折，恢复桡骨头对合；检查桡骨头稳定性与骨间后神经。Monteggia: reduce and fix the ulna fracture, restore radial head congruity; check radial head stability and the posterior interosseous nerve.
  • Galeazzi：复位固定桡骨骨折，评估 DRUJ 稳定性；DRUJ 不稳定需同期处理。术后将前臂置于 DRUJ 最稳定的旋转体位制动以保护韧带愈合，旋转锻炼待稳定性确认后再开始。Galeazzi: reduce and fix the radius fracture, assess DRUJ stability; unstable DRUJ needs concurrent management. Post-operatively, splint the forearm in the rotational position where the DRUJ is most stable to protect ligament healing; rotational exercises only after stability is confirmed.
  • 两者都不是“单纯前臂骨折”，漏诊即治疗错误。Neither is "just a forearm fracture" — missing the diagnosis means wrong treatment.

桡骨远端Distal radius按关节受累与稳定性分层Stratify by articular involvement & stability

  • 关节外、稳定：闭合复位 + 石膏/支具，多可保守。Extra-articular and stable: closed reduction + cast/brace; often non-operative.
  • 关节内明显移位或整体不稳定：多需手术恢复关节面。Markedly displaced intra-articular fractures or overall instability: usually operative to restore the articular surface.
  • DRUJ 受累本身不等于手术指征：仅受累而稳定者可保守并随访；持续不稳定或移位者，结合年龄、功能需求、关节面情况与整体稳定性综合决定。DRUJ involvement alone is not a surgical indication: stable involvement may be managed non-operatively with follow-up; persistent instability or displacement calls for an individualised decision integrating age, functional demands, articular status and overall stability.
  • 复位后复查：X 线对位、DRUJ、腕管症状。Post-reduction recheck: radiographic alignment, DRUJ, carpal tunnel symptoms.

4.6　并发症 / 随访4.6　Complications / Follow-up

  • 旋转功能受限：前臂骨折最特征的并发症，康复重点。Loss of rotation: the signature complication of forearm fractures; the focus of rehabilitation.
  • 骨不连 / 畸形愈合：尤其双骨折对线不良者。Nonunion / malunion: especially with poor alignment in both-bone fractures.
  • DRUJ 不稳定、创伤性关节炎（腕关节）。DRUJ instability, post-traumatic wrist arthritis.
  • 肌腱粘连、骨间膜挛缩、神经症状残留。Tendon adhesions, interosseous membrane contracture, residual neurologic symptoms.
  • 随访：X 线愈合、旋转活动度、DRUJ 稳定性、神经恢复。Follow-up: radiographic healing, rotational motion, DRUJ stability, nerve recovery.

4.7　病例自测4.7　Case Self-Test

病例 4-1（分段揭晓）Case 4-1 (step-by-step reveal)

注：本病例为合成教学病例，非真实患者。Note: synthetic teaching case, not a real patient.

30 岁男性，跌倒后左前臂剧痛、畸形。逐步揭晓。A 30-year-old man has severe left forearm pain and deformity after a fall. Reveal step by step.

步骤 1 · 查体Step 1 · Exam
前臂中段畸形，皮肤完整，桡动脉搏动可及。按铁律查肘查腕：肘部压痛不明显，腕部尺侧压痛明显、DRUJ 区饱满。Mid-forearm deformity; skin intact; radial pulse palpable. Per the unit rule, check elbow and wrist: elbow non-tender, but marked ulnar-sided wrist tenderness with a prominent DRUJ.
步骤 2 · 影像Step 2 · Imaging
前臂正侧位（含肘腕）：桡骨干中段骨折、成角移位；腕关节 DRUJ 间隙增宽、尺骨头向背侧半脱位。诊断：Galeazzi 损伤。Forearm AP/lateral including elbow and wrist: midshaft radial fracture with angulation; widened DRUJ with dorsal subluxation of the ulnar head. Diagnosis: Galeazzi injury.
步骤 3 · 决策Step 3 · Decision
这不是单纯桡骨骨折。手术复位固定桡骨，术中评估 DRUJ 稳定性；若 DRUJ 不稳定，同期稳定 DRUJ。术后将前臂置于 DRUJ 最稳定的旋转体位制动（控制旋转，促进韧带愈合），旋转锻炼待稳定性确认后再开始；若 DRUJ 明显不稳定需克氏针临时固定，则制动期间禁止前臂旋转。Not just a radial fracture. Operative reduction and fixation of the radius with intra-operative DRUJ stability assessment; stabilise the DRUJ concurrently if unstable. Post-operatively, splint the forearm in the rotational position where the DRUJ is most stable (controlling rotation to allow ligament healing); begin rotational exercises only after stability is confirmed. If gross DRUJ instability requires temporary K-wire transfixation, forearm rotation is prohibited during immobilisation.
步骤 4 · 教训Step 4 · Lesson
若只看前臂正侧位中央段、X 线未包含腕关节，DRUJ 脱位将被漏诊，单纯固定桡骨会导致腕关节慢性不稳定。Had the radiograph not included the wrist, the DRUJ dislocation would have been missed, and fixing only the radius would leave chronic wrist instability.

Q1. 前臂 X 线显示单纯尺骨干骨折，接下来的关键动作是？Q1. Forearm radiographs show an isolated ulnar shaft fracture. What is the key next step?

答案：BAnswer: B
尺骨骨折 + 桡骨头脱位 = Monteggia。单骨骨折必须看相邻关节，这是前臂创伤的铁律。Ulna fracture + radial head dislocation = Monteggia. Single-bone fractures demand assessment of the adjacent joint — the iron rule of forearm trauma.

Q2. 桡骨远端骨折复位固定后，患者诉拇指、示指进行性麻木、胀痛加重，应首先考虑？Q2. After reduction and fixation of a distal radius fracture, the patient reports progressive thumb/index numbness and worsening swelling pain. First consideration?

答案：DAnswer: D
进行性正中神经分布区麻木 + 疼痛加重是红旗，需立即评估腕管压力与前臂骨筋膜室，必要时紧急减压。等待观察可致不可逆损伤。Progressive median-nerve numbness with worsening pain is a red flag — urgently assess carpal tunnel pressure and forearm compartments; decompress emergently if needed. Waiting risks irreversible damage.
