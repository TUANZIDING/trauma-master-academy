# Orthopaedics teaching pilot / 骨科创伤教学试用

## Scope / 范围
12 adult injury units: cervical/thoracic/lumbar fractures, shoulder/elbow dislocation, humerus/radius-ulna fractures, hip dislocation, femur/patella/tibial plateau/ankle fractures.

Muse authored the course pages in four parallel chats. The first simplified anatomy illustrations and their derived videos were rejected during visual review and are excluded from the release. Codex independently inspected the delivered files, returned corrections, integrated navigation and checked the website. Muse cloud attachments were used; its native Mac file-write connection did not pass the connection test.

## Evidence and review status / 证据与审核状态
This is an AI-assisted teaching pilot, pending human specialist/teacher review. Source links and selected high-risk statements were checked against AO Surgery Reference, AO Spine, AAOS OrthoInfo and cited original research. This is not an exhaustive guideline review, clinical certification or human specialist sign-off. AO names are references, not endorsements.

本版为 AI 辅助教学试用稿，待教师或相关专科医生复核。公开来源与部分高风险表述已核对，不代表全部医学断言逐条完成系统审查。病例练习为合成教学情境；引用的公开影像独立标注原始来源，不视为合成病例的真实检查结果。

## Corrections incorporated / 修订重点
- Protected rehabilitation after Galeazzi injury; no blanket immediate forearm rotation.
- Adult elbow sources instead of pediatric timing extrapolation.
- Individualized humeral-shaft and distal radioulnar-joint decisions; classification alone is not an indication.
- Humeral-head blood supply and strict selection limits for nonoperative terrible-triad management.
- AO Spine anterior/posterior tension-band distinction and lumbar cord/cauda-equina localization.
- Tibial-plateau staged care tied to soft-tissue and patient status, not a skin-wrinkle-only rule.
- Withdrawal of inadequate invented anatomy images; replacement with source-backed published figures and bilingual observation notes.

## Design reference / 设计参考
[OIOI Academy medical learning](https://oioi-academy.com/medical-learning/): observe, understand, review; staged explanation; bilingual terminology; explicit source/model limits. The implementation and media are independent; no third-party website assets or AO videos were copied.

## Media / 媒体
Use published images only when their individual reuse terms have been verified. Image credits identify the original source and license; translated teaching notes are separate from the source caption. Public availability alone does not grant reuse rights. Different patients or source publications must not be presented as one clinical sequence.

[AO Surgery Reference FAQ](https://surgeryreference.aofoundation.org/further-reading/faq) permits illustrations in noncommercial presentations with the AO logo and source attribution, but requires separate permission for reproduction online. This public site therefore provides direct AO reference-page links rather than redistributing its images without permission. Video resources link to verified official destinations; the rejected diagram-based videos are not included.

## Release checks / 发布检查（2026-10-04）
27 source figures: 6 spine, 7 upper limb, 14 lower limb (including 3 folded comparison figures). Per-image provenance is in assets/media_sources*.csv. Original image pixels remain unaltered; CSS scales them without cropping and full-size views are linked. Five public-domain Gray anatomy plates are historical anatomy references, not current treatment guidance.

AO figures remain on their official source pages. Karthik 2011 is CC BY 2.0 (corrected from an erroneous 4.0 link). Cureus sources that state CC BY without a version are not labeled 4.0. Cervical, thoracic and lumbar image placement and the Chinese translation of coronoid process (冠状突) were corrected during independent review.

Validation: repository npm run validate; additional local-link/anchor, image-alt and inline-JavaScript checks. Native Chrome reviewed desktop and 400px responsive layouts, language switching and quiz feedback in all three course pages. No AI-created anatomy media or derived MP4 is included in this module.
