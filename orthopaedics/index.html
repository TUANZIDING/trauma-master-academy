学习方法How to learn

每个单元都按三段推进：分阶段展开，文字只给关键层，需要时再深入。Each unit follows three stages: content unfolds step by step — key points first, details on demand.

1先观察Observe

先看解剖与影像：这块骨头长什么样、断在哪里、关节对位丢失多少。Start with anatomy and images: what the bone looks like, where it broke, how much joint alignment is lost.

2再理解Understand

再看机制与分型：分型辅助沟通，结合稳定性、神经血管与软组织状态比较治疗选择。Then mechanism and classification: classifications aid communication; compare treatment options using stability, neurovascular status, and soft-tissue condition.

3再复盘Review

最后自测与复盘：用自测题检验是否真正掌握，错题回看前两段。Finish with a self-quiz and review: check what you really know, then loop back to the earlier stages for anything you missed.

开放骨折：抗生素、清创与覆盖Open fracture: antibiotics, debridement and coverage

抗生素依据并列Antibiotic sources side by side

尽早静脉抗生素，尽量伤后1 h内。国内2019 Gustilo I–II以一代头孢（原文头孢唑啉）为基础；III加氨基糖苷，粪便/厌氧污染加青霉素或甲硝唑；过敏/肾功能及本地耐药谱决定替代。以本院规范为准。Early IV antibiotics, ideally within one hour. Chinese2019 GustiloI–II first-generation cephalosporin(cefazolin), III adds aminoglycoside, fecal/anaerobic contamination adds penicillin/metronidazole; allergy/renal function/local resistance guide alternatives. Follow institutional policy.

国内2019持续至闭合后48 h；不能当所有现代方案统一疗程，按本院抗菌管理选择。盐水湿敷+封闭层与授权照片；BOAST/NICE不做急诊小冲洗，国内旧文急诊清洗说法另需本院定标。以本院规范为准。Chinese2019 continues until48 hours after closure, not a universal modern duration; use local stewardship. Saline-moist/occlusive dressing and authorized photo; BOAST/NICE no ED mini-washout differs from older Chinese cleaning text. Follow institutional policy.

清创与重建Debridement and reconstruction

重污染/血管受损立即；其他高能12 h内、低能24 h内清创；确定覆盖尽量72 h内，内固定需与覆盖配合。破伤风与多区域复评同步。以本院规范为准。Immediate debridement for severe contamination/vascular compromise; other high-energy within12 hours/low-energy within24; definitive coverage ideally within72, coordinated with fixation. Include tetanus and whole-patient review. Follow institutional policy.

筋膜室：早诊与立即减压Compartment syndrome: early diagnosis and immediate decompression

有脉搏也可缺血A pulse can coexist with ischemia

高风险每小时查体，记录疼痛/被动牵拉痛及镇痛剂量；有症状松解环形层至皮肤，心脏水平，30 min内复评并即时骨科会诊。以本院规范为准。High risk: hourly exam, pain/passive stretch/analgesic dose. Symptoms: release circumferential layers to skin, heart-level limb, reassess within30 minutes and consult orthopedics immediately. Follow institutional policy.

DBP−间室压<30 mmHg风险增加；绝对压>40 mmHg考虑紧急减压，不能脱离临床。确诊后立即切开；国内2019“6–8 h内”是旧表述，不能作为等待理由。以本院规范为准。DBP minus compartment pressure below30 increases risk; absolute over40 prompts urgent decompression assessment in context. Once diagnosed, immediate fasciotomy; older Chinese2019 wording within6–8 hours is not permission to delay. Follow institutional policy.

硬征与紧急复流Hard signs and urgent reperfusion

无脉、持续出血、扩张性血肿为硬征；毛细血管再充盈或Doppler不能单独排除。立即骨科/血管/整形协同，必要时临时分流。以本院规范为准。Absent pulse/ongoing bleeding/expanding hematoma are hard signs; CRT/Doppler alone cannot exclude injury. Immediate orthopedic/vascular/plastic coordination, temporary shunt when needed. Follow institutional policy.
