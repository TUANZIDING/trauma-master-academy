课前测 · 5 题Pretest · five questions

4. 实习医生遇到超出能力的侵入操作，应怎样参与？How should an intern participate in an invasive task beyond their competence?
5. 交接时哪些信息最容易被遗漏？Which information is easily lost at handover?

学习方法How to learn

每个单元都按三段推进：分阶段展开，文字只给关键层，需要时再深入。Each unit follows three stages: content unfolds step by step — key points first, details on demand.

1先观察Observe

先看解剖与影像：这块骨头长什么样、断在哪里、关节对位丢失多少。Start with anatomy and images: what the bone looks like, where it broke, how much joint alignment is lost.

2再理解Understand

再看机制与分型：分型辅助沟通，结合稳定性、神经血管与软组织状态比较治疗选择。Then mechanism and classification: classifications aid communication; compare treatment options using stability, neurovascular status, and soft-tissue condition.

3再复盘Review

最后自测与复盘：用自测题检验是否真正掌握，错题回看前两段。Finish with a self-quiz and review: check what you really know, then loop back to the earlier stages for anything you missed.

课后测 · 5 题Posttest · five questions

每题 1 分；用于形成性反馈。One point per item; formative feedback.

1. 临时固定后疼痛加剧、远端感觉改变，复评重点是什么？Pain and distal sensory change follow splinting. What must be reassessed?
2. 交接一个已固定的肢体，需传递哪些未解问题？Which unresolved issues belong in handover of a stabilized limb?
3. 患者表现改变后，哪种报告最便于团队行动？After the patient changes, which report best supports team action?
4. 规培学员已练过模拟操作，真实患者上怎样执行？A resident has practised on a simulator. How is real-patient performance governed?
5. 检查尚未最终报告，交接应怎样处理？A test has no final report yet. What belongs in handover?
