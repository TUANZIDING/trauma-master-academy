第一次进入创伤抢救室First day in the trauma bay

先看懂房间、站对位置，再开口说第一句话。Read the room, take your place, then speak.

学完你能You will be able to

  • 认出抢救室分区与关键站位：气道、操作、记录、组长Identify bay zones and key positions: airway, procedures, scribe, leader
  • 用固定格式听懂院前交接（ATMIST）Receive pre-hospital handover in a fixed format (ATMIST)
  • 患者到达前完成团队简报，明确组长与分工Run a pre-arrival team briefing with a named leader and roles

临床锚点Clinical anchor

一个组长、一个声音：角色在患者进门前分配好，闭环沟通确认每条指令。One leader, one voice: assign roles before arrival and close the loop on every instruction.

从院前预警到归处交接，每一环都有责任人。From pre-alert to disposition, every link has an owner.

03核心Core45 分钟min

看懂 xABCDE 创伤初评xABCDE primary survey

先止住会致命的出血，再按顺序一层层排除威胁。Stop the bleeding that kills first, then clear threats in order.

学完你能You will be able to

  • 按 x→A→B→C→D→E 发现并当场处理致命威胁Find and treat life threats in x→A→B→C→D→E order
  • 每次干预后、病情变化时回到 x 重新评估Return to x after every intervention or change
  • 分清初评与二次评估的边界Separate the primary from the secondary survey

临床锚点Clinical anchor

ATLS 第 11 版（2025）把 ABCDE 改为 xABCDE：致命性外出血先控制——直接压迫、止血带、伤口填塞。ATLS 11th ed. (2025) made ABCDE into xABCDE: control exsanguinating external haemorrhage first with pressure, tourniquet or packing.

04专题Focus45 分钟min

骨盆创伤临床思维Pelvic trauma reasoning

骨盆里能藏下大量失血，看不见不等于没有。The pelvis can hide massive bleeding; unseen is not absent.

学完你能You will be able to

  • 识别血流动力学不稳定的骨盆骨折Recognise haemodynamically unstable pelvic fractures
  • 正确放置骨盆带并确认效果Apply a pelvic binder correctly and confirm its effect
  • 按分级路径决定填塞、栓塞或手术，启动 MDTChoose packing, angioembolisation or surgery by pathway, with MDT

临床锚点Clinical anchor

骨盆带以股骨大转子为中心，而不是髂嵴；怀疑骨盆骨折出血即尽早使用。Centre the binder on the greater trochanters, not the iliac crests; apply early when pelvic bleeding is suspected.

05专题Focus180 分钟min

骨科创伤：骨折与脱位Orthopaedics: fractures and dislocations

十二个成人单元，先保肢体，再谈对位。Twelve adult units: save the limb before alignment.

学完你能You will be able to

  • 复位或上夹板前后，各查一次远端神经血管Check distal neurovascular status before and after reduction or splinting
  • 认出四类急症：开放骨折、血管损伤、骨筋膜室综合征、难复脱位Spot four emergencies: open fracture, vascular injury, compartment syndrome, irreducible dislocation
  • 按部位比较解剖结构与稳定性Compare anatomy and stability by region

临床锚点Clinical anchor

开放骨折：伤后 1 小时内静脉抗生素；伤口拍照后用生理盐水敷料覆盖，避免反复暴露。Open fractures: IV antibiotics within 1 hour; photograph, then cover with saline-soaked dressing and avoid re-exposure.

06系统System180 分钟min

创伤急救与灾难医学Trauma and disaster medicine

伤员多于资源时，目标从救一个人变成救最多的人。When casualties exceed resources, save the most, not just one.

学完你能You will be able to

  • 切换到批量伤员思维：指挥、通信、资源Switch to mass-casualty thinking: command, communication, resources
  • 用 START 快速检伤分类：红、黄、绿、黑Triage fast with START: red, yellow, green, black
  • 四个学时，最后一课综合推演Four lessons ending in a full simulation

临床锚点Clinical anchor

START：能走为绿；呼吸 >30 次/分、桡动脉搏动消失或再充盈 >2 秒、不能听从指令，任一项即为红。START: walking is green; RR >30, absent radial pulse or refill >2 s, or cannot follow commands means red.
