病种迷你模块矩阵Disease Mini-Module Matrix

四个病种迷你模块与三个脱敏教学病例共同构成可点击矩阵。每个模块逐步升级为“机制—动态查体—监护趋势—影像讨论—处置—循证来源”的完整学习单元。 Four disease mini modules and three de-identified teaching cases form a clickable matrix. Each module is being upgraded into a complete unit spanning mechanism, dynamic examination, monitor trends, imaging discussion, management, and traceable evidence.

1 · 机制与病理生理1 · Mechanism and physiology

解释能量、方向、解剖和时间如何产生损伤。Explain how energy, direction, anatomy, and time create injury.

2 · 动态查体与监护2 · Dynamic exam and monitoring

至少两轮查体和三时点趋势，明确与前次相比的变化。At least two examination rounds and three trend points, always compared with prior.

3 · 影像团队讨论3 · Imaging team discussion

说明看哪里、回答什么、不能排除什么，以及如何与生理整合。State where to look, what is answered, what is not excluded, and how it fits physiology.

4 · 循证处置与复评4 · Evidence-based care and reassessment

用团队决策框架展示支持、升级和专科资源，并链接权威指南。Show support, escalation, and specialty resources as a team framework with authoritative links.

医生主导、证据约束的制作流程Clinician-led, evidence-constrained workflow

视觉帮助学习，不作为医学证据；医学主张的来源、适用范围与审批决定均保留在可审计台账中。Visuals support learning but are not evidence; source, scope, and approval decisions for medical claims are retained in an auditable register.

1. Source index

从公开指南入口和本地教学资料索引开始。Start from public guideline entry points and local teaching-source indexes.

2. Claim mapping

把教学陈述拆成 claim、来源和审核状态。Split teaching statements into claims, sources, and review statuses.

3. Visual courseware

用图文解释空间、流程、团队和复评。Use visuals to teach space, flow, teamwork, and reassessment.

4. Independent gates

逐条核对证据位置、双语语义、临床表述、本院适配、隐私授权和发布范围。Review evidence location, bilingual meaning, clinical wording, local adaptation, privacy authorization, and release scope independently.
