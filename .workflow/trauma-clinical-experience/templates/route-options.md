# Route Options

## Shared constraints

- Educational use only.
- No unsupported thresholds, doses, device specifications, or bedside algorithms.
- No real-patient payload enters the public repository before de-identification and authorization.
- No protected course text or figure is copied.

## Route A: structure-only case

- Uses synthetic or abstracted information.
- Lowest privacy risk.
- Suitable when source conflicts or authorization remain open.

## Route B: de-identified instructor preview

- Uses approved derived facts and reviewed anonymous visuals.
- Requires documented de-identification and teaching authorization.
- Static instructor mode is presentation control, not security.

## Route C: student release

- Requires atomized claims, source locations, bilingual review, privacy authorization, and explicit release scope.
- Unsupported claims are removed or remain reviewer-only.
