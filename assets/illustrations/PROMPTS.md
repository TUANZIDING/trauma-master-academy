# Vivid course illustration prompt record

Status: `visual_asset_pending_review`

These images are teaching illustrations, not diagnostic images or procedural guidance. They use anonymous simulation mannequins, contain no real patient identity, and intentionally avoid blood, gore, dosage, thresholds, readable monitor data, logos, and embedded instructional text.

Shared visual direction: 16:10 landscape editorial medical education illustration; East Asian clinical teams; vivid semi-realistic digital painting; teal, cream, slate, and restrained coral palette; cinematic depth; warm clinical light.

## Asset prompts

- `trauma-bay-vivid.png`: modern trauma bay orientation, coordinated team around an empty simulation mannequin, generic ultrasound and monitor forms.
- `trauma-care-chain-vivid.png`: coherent care chain from roadside response and ambulance transport through trauma-bay handoff to rehabilitation.
- `pelvic-trauma-vivid.png`: team reassessment with a translucent pelvic-bone teaching overlay and abstract ultrasound information node.
- `tbi-mini-vivid.png`: neurologic reassessment with a translucent brain silhouette suggesting consciousness, pupils, and evolving status.

- `abdominal-injury-mini-vivid.png`: hidden-injury reasoning with translucent liver, spleen, and fluid-space cues plus an abstract ultrasound screen.
- `polytrauma-mini-vivid.png`: structured whole-body reassessment with connected head, chest, abdomen, pelvis, and limb risk nodes.
- `trauma-disaster-vivid.png`: simulated earthquake field coordination area showing command, triage flow, resource allocation, and safe transfer.
- `cspine-hip-case-vivid.png`: competing cervical-spine and hip concerns shown as separated translucent overlays during team priority discussion.
- `abdominal-shock-case-vivid.png`: occult abdominal hemorrhage reasoning, serial perfusion comparison, and resource communication without visible external bleeding.
