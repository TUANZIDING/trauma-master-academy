# Trauma Skills Academy V1 scope addendum

Status: `pending_human_approval`

This addendum records a proposed expansion from clinical-reasoning courseware into supervised procedural-skills simulation. It does not alter or supersede `docs/CLINICAL_SAFETY_BOUNDARY.md`.

## Permitted in instructor preview

- nine-skill curriculum map;
- learning objectives and simulation-station domains;
- source/version/scope locators;
- non-executable visual and video storyboards;
- synthetic case prompts focused on observation, communication, effect checks, and reassessment;
- formative OSCE domains without an uncalibrated universal pass score.

## Withheld from learner release

- device specifications, drug regimens, and patient-specific settings;
- executable technique sequences that could be used without supervised training;
- institution-specific activation, transfer, and escalation pathways;
- claims of clinical approval before a controlled signoff record exists;
- copied content from protected certification manuals.

## Approval gates

1. Each `CLAIM-TSA-*` record receives a retain/revise/return decision from a trauma chief physician.
2. Local equipment, roles, monitoring, imaging, transfer, and escalation records are mapped.
3. Each simulation station is dry-run with an instructor observation form.
4. Critical-error items and scoring are calibrated before summative use.
5. Visual anatomy, privacy, copyright, and bilingual terminology are reviewed.

## Rollback

If a source is withdrawn, a claim conflicts with local policy, a visual is anatomically misleading, or approval evidence is absent, the affected skill returns to instructor preview and is removed from learner navigation.
