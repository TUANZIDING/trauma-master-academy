# Trauma Disaster Case Plan

## Case 1

ID: `CASE-TRAUMA-DISASTER-SYN-001`  
Use: Lesson 1 and Lesson 2  
Status: `synthetic_no_real_patient`, `pending_clinician_review`

Scenario: 32-year-old male after high-speed traffic injury, with agitation, rapid breathing, and active right lower-limb bleeding reported before arrival.

Learner tasks:

- Trauma team activation.
- Role assignment.
- Equipment readiness language.
- Blood product readiness as team-resource discussion.
- Initial priority statement.
- Reassessment after new information.

Unsafe path: selecting whole-body CT before shared team assessment. Feedback uses delay and trend concern language only.

## Case 2

ID: `CASE-TRAUMA-DISASTER-SYN-002`  
Use: Lesson 3 and Lesson 4  
Status: `synthetic_no_real_patient`, `pending_clinician_review`

Scenario: public-space explosion with partial structural collapse. First wave includes 8 expected casualties and unclear later arrival burden.

Synthetic patients:

- P1: active major bleeding and shock risk.
- P2: breathing difficulty and unilateral breath-sound concern.
- P3: stable closed fracture.
- P4: minor injury and able to walk.
- P5: severe traumatic brain injury concern and high resource need.
- P6: abdominal injury concern with later deterioration.

System resources:

- Casualties.
- Resuscitation bays.
- Operating rooms.
- Blood products.
- Available teams.
- Ambulances.
- ICU beds.
- Transfer capacity.

Boundary: the case teaches resource reasoning and reassessment, not a triage algorithm, treatment protocol, or local hospital command policy.
