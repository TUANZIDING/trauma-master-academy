# Clinical Safety Boundary

This project is an educational courseware project.

It must not become:

- clinical decision support;
- a diagnosis system;
- a prescription tool;
- a procedural guide;
- emergency advice;
- a medical device.

## Allowed Teaching Language

Use phrases such as:

- learners should identify the risk and report it to the instructor or team;
- this equipment or exam may appear as a communication node in a trauma workflow;
- specific imaging, stabilization, airway management, and treatment decisions are made by qualified clinical teams under local policy;
- cases are synthetic or de-identified teaching materials pending review.

## Forbidden Language

Avoid:

- immediate treatment commands;
- if-this-then-that clinical algorithms;
- drug doses;
- oxygen flow rates or ventilator settings;
- device sizes;
- GCS, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, lactate, urine output, or other thresholds as action triggers;
- indications, contraindications, and complication-management instructions;
- claims that content is clinician approved unless real signoff exists.

## Review Status

Keep conservative statuses visible:

- `source_required`
- `source_index_pending_review`
- `evidence_review_required`
- `pending_clinician_review`
- `pending_deidentification_review`
- `pending_local_confirmation`
