# Trauma Disaster Learning Objectives

## Lesson 1

创伤急救总论与 xABCDE 思维 / Trauma Care Principles and the xABCDE Approach

- Explain why trauma teaching emphasizes priority language.
- Use xABCDE as shared observation and reporting language.
- Describe Observe, Report, Coordinate, and Reassess as a cycle.
- Identify trauma team roles and learner position without turning roles into local policy.
- State why reassessment follows new information and team action.

## Lesson 2

立即致命创伤与早期复苏 / Immediate Life Threats and Early Trauma Resuscitation

- Link x, A, B, C, D, and E to learner observation tasks.
- Report changing patient trends without using treatment thresholds.
- Recognize that early resource selection should not displace immediate threat recognition.
- Explain What happened, Why it happened, What the team would discuss, and What must be reassessed.

## Lesson 3

灾难医学总论与批量伤员响应 / Principles of Disaster Medicine and Mass-Casualty Response

- Define disaster teaching as demand-resource imbalance rather than a single algorithm.
- Identify scene safety, incident command, communication, transport, and resource allocation as system nodes.
- Use mass-casualty triage as a classroom sorting concept pending course-lead confirmation.
- Describe hospital surge response without asserting local activation rules.

## Lesson 4

创伤与灾难医学综合推演 / Integrated Trauma and Disaster Simulation

- Activate a classroom response and assign roles.
- Sort six synthetic patients by concern and resource need.
- Allocate limited resources as a discussion exercise.
- Reassess patient and system priorities after new information.
- Conduct a structured handover using known, unknown, concern, and next reassessment.
