## Source-qualified teaching and excluded material

Doses, device sizes, oxygen or ventilator settings, numerical triggers and conditional treatment paths are permitted as teaching content. Each must identify a supporting source, applicable population, clinical context, exceptions and institutional-policy dependence. Interns focus on recognition and reporting; residents practise within supervision and authorization; attendings coordinate the team, resources and reassessment. A displayed reference does not grant clinical authorization.

Excluded material includes invented local activation rules, unsupported numerical prescriptions, real-patient inputs used to generate unsupervised care commands, and identifiable patient details. Real-case adaptations must keep recorded facts separate from conditional teaching paths and must not reconstruct missing measurements or outcomes.
