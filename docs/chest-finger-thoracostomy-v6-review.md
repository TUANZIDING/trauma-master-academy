# Finger thoracostomy update — 2026-10-04

Scope: new `#cm6-finger` within existing `#management`, scoped CSS and four licensed original illustrations. No other course content, cases, measurements or scoring edited.

ETM evidence: historical 2017 brochure explicitly lists needle/finger/tube thoracostomy; public Thoracotomy Critique discusses finger followed by tube. This confirms inclusion in historical public ETM teaching, not verification of the current paid manual or every item of its older critique. Older critique is provenance only, not current thoracotomy indications or CPR guidance.

Clinical context: NICE NG39 1.3.4–1.3.6 and 1.4.1–1.4.2 supports decompression in suspected tension with severe compromise/instability, open thoracostomy when expertise exists and subsequent tube drainage. UK Defence Medical Command Thoracostomy reviewed 2026-04-01 supplies procedural mechanics and ventilation-specific aftercare. Its deployed-setting bilateral/failed-needle/dimension rules are not promoted as universal hospital rules. Ordinary hypoxia, chest pain or flail chest alone is not an indication. No claim of universal superiority or mortality benefit.
