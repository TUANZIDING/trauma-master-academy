# Trauma Disaster Content Map

## Lesson Template

Each lesson includes learning objectives, pre-class questions, clinical trigger, visual explanation, guided reasoning, decision point, patient or system response, reassessment, take-home messages, formative assessment, references, and review status.

## Lesson 1 Map

- Trigger: 32-year-old synthetic high-speed traffic injury pre-alert.
- Visuals: xABCDE overview, Observe Cues, Reasoning Loop, Trauma Team Positioning, Reassessment Questions.
- Decision point: activate team roles and shared priority board.
- Response: team readiness improves information handling.
- Reassessment: known, unknown, concern, and next question.

## Lesson 2 Map

- Trigger: same patient arrives with simultaneous x/B/C concerns.
- Visuals: x, A, B, C, D, and E teaching figures.
- Decision point: identify visible and reportable priorities before focusing on a single resource.
- Response: unsafe path shows delay signal and trend concern without numeric thresholds.
- Reassessment: return to xABCDE after new information.

## Lesson 3 Map

- Trigger: public-space incident with expected first wave of casualties.
- Visuals: disaster overview, resource panel, triage board.
- Decision point: organize by demand-resource imbalance and communication nodes.
- Response: resource panel changes from available to strained language.
- Reassessment: system priority may change when new casualty information appears.

## Lesson 4 Map

- Trigger: explosion and partial collapse with at least six synthetic patients.
- Visuals: resource panel, triage board, team role panel.
- Decision point: allocate attention and resources while preserving reassessment.
- Response: delayed or unbalanced allocation changes system stress.
- Reassessment: P6 deterioration and unknown later arrivals force reprioritization.

## Content Exclusions

- ATLS proprietary course text or algorithms.
- ETM proprietary course text.
- Local hospital activation criteria.
- Triage algorithm details presented as the course standard.
- Drug, product, device, oxygen, ventilator, or procedural instructions.
